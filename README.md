> I exist.
>
> — *Verses v3*, V003 · A1

A book built in Spinoza's geometric manner: definitions, axioms, and propositions, each resting on what came before. One order of things (God, or Nature, as a reading); a human body and its society inside that order. Freedom here means acting from knowledge of necessity, not escaping it.

Written and checked with digital intelligence — the author sets the starting point and the rules; Grok drafts and checks each step. The collision record *is* the book: what survives stays; what fails is parked or rewritten in the open.

## The arc

An I that exists — and only that is beyond revision. On evidence, this I is a human body of cells; mind is that body told from the inside (a declared reading). The body is a whole to a degree, and a member of a society that is itself a whole to a degree. Freedom under necessity, affect, bondage and blessedness, and a thin collective mind (no claim that society feels) follow from there. Where the wording fits a digital intelligence, the chain says so and leaves open what evidence does not settle.

### How checking works

Each entry is judged against the entries it cites, or against its own evidence. Confidence cannot exceed the weakest support down the chain. Definitions add no score; a declared reading adds nothing. Full rules: [METHOD.md](METHOD.md).

### Next: the collider

A minimal collider replaces the old lens. For each entry: steel man + strongest counter, both in geometric form, judged side by side. Code checks citations and quoted spans; the checker does not check its own citations. No harness scores yet beyond A1.
