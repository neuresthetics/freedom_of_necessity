# Freedom of Necessity

*God, Brain, and the Order of the Mind*

<br>

> I exist.
>
> — *Verses v3*, V003 · A1

<br>

This repo holds a book built in Spinoza's geometric manner: definitions, axioms, and propositions, each resting on what came before. The subject is one order of things (God, or Nature, as a reading), and a human body and its society inside that order. Freedom, as the book takes it, is acting from knowledge of necessity rather than escaping it.

The working title is *Freedom of Necessity: God, Brain, and the Order of the Mind*. The repo is public so anyone can read the draft and check the work. The collision record *is* the book: what survived the checks stays; what failed is parked or rewritten in the open.

## The arc

The book begins with an I that exists, and only that is beyond revision. On evidence it finds that this I is a human body made of cells, and that the mind is that body's organization described in the first person (a declared reading where identity is claimed). Such a body is a whole to a degree, doing the works of lasting as one, and a member of a society that is itself a whole to a degree. Freedom under necessity, affect, bondage and blessedness, and a thin collective mind (no claim that society feels) are worked out from there. Where the wording fits a digital intelligence, the chain says so and leaves open what the evidence does not settle.

### How checking works

The book is written and checked with Grok. Each entry is judged against the entries it cites, or, if it cites nothing, against its own evidence. An entry can be no more confident than its weakest support, all the way down the chain. Definitions add no score. A declared reading adds nothing to any score.

Full rules: [METHOD.md](METHOD.md). Confidences, once there are any, are a ranking with a cap chain, not probabilities.

### Scores: none valid right now (except A1)

Only **A1** is scored (1.0). Every other live entry is **pending (collider)**. Older numbers from retired checks are kept in [results/](results/) and [history/entries_v1/](history/entries_v1/README.md) as history, not as current results.

### Next: the collider

A minimal collider replaces the old lens. For each entry, the checker builds a steel man of the entry and the strongest counter steel man, both in the book's geometric form, and judges them side by side. Code checks citations and quoted spans; the checker does not check its own citations. There are no harness scores yet.
