## Decisions (with rejects)

### D1 — Id scheme
- **Chose:** `A5`, `D-free`, `P3`.

- **Rejected:** folder-only `A-cause` as the file id (kept as plain-language alias in logs); `C-FREE-*` prefix (hard-problem draft uses `C-HP-` because those are not promotion-shaped).

### D2 — Kind of A5
- **Chose:** `type: axiom`, `kind: model`.
- **Why:** like A3/A4 — a working claim with a stated failure mode; confidence pending.
- **Rejected:**
  - `self-evident` — uncaused events are conceivable; A1's certainty pattern does not apply.
  - `empirical` — no lesion-style evidence chain analogous to A2(a).
  - `declared reading` — METHOD rule 6 would make anything capped only through A5 unscored; verse 135 is ‡ and "can be lowered," which needs a lowerable claim.
  - promoting E1P29 itself — stronger than the book needs (v12 note; v10/v11 entry 13 notes).

### D3 — Scope of A5
- **Chose:** every event has a cause; choices included; weaker than E1P29; uncaused events at choice scale lower it and yield chance, not freedom.
- **Why:** matches v12 note to 135 and v10/v11 "Axiom F3" notes.
- **Rejected:** building quantum mechanics into the axiom text (belongs in "what would lower it," not in the claim).

### D4 — D-free wording
- **Chose:** two definitions — strict (E1D7, Nature only) and degree (organization + knowledge of causes). Could-have-done-otherwise excluded from both.
- **Why:** D-Nature Scholium 4 already holds strict sense; v11 glossary ("freedom = degree of adequate causation"); verses 140–144, 143.
- **Rejected:** single definition that blurs strict and degree; defining freedom as "adequate causation" alone without the knowledge clause (v11 note to 95: understanding *raises* the degree, and verse 144 makes knowledge part of the author's position); citing E4P66S as if it *defined* degrees (it names the free man vs slave — report only).

### D5 — P3 statement
- **Chose:** compatibility of degree-freedom with A5; freer by degree from organization + knowledge; CHDO not required.
- **Why:** answers the review's "no derived proposition"; keeps one chain (no split track for "metaphysical freedom" vs "political freedom").
- **Rejected:** a freedom entry that ignores A5 (would leave 135‡); deriving ultimate responsibility (verse 146† denies it); scoring any confidence number.

### D6 — What is derived vs declared in P3
