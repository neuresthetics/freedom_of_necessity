# Collision: what freedom requires

**Status.** Candidate collision, written 2026-10-17 PT. It scores nothing and changes nothing in `entries/`. Outcome: **unscored, collider not run.** Falsifiers were recorded before each side's argument (see [COLLISION_LOG.md](COLLISION_LOG.md)).

**The question.** The book proposes D-free (degree) and P3: freedom is acting from one's own organization and from knowledge of causes, compatible with every event being caused (A5); could-have-done-otherwise is not required. Is that freedom, or a relabeling?

Three theses:

- **L (libertarian, Kane-style).** Freedom worth the name requires that the agent could have done otherwise, given the same total prior state; some choices are undetermined in a way that still belongs to the agent.
- **H (hard incompatibilist, Pereboom-style).** If every event is caused, the book's "freedom" is only a polite name for caused behavior plus insight; it cannot ground desert, and knowledge does not make the agent the source in the sense that matters.
- **B (book).** D-free and P3: freedom by degree under necessity; could-have-done-otherwise not required; a caused choice can still be the agent's.

**Rules.** Each side at full strength. Each point names what it stands on. No scores. Spinoza cited by part and proposition only, checked against `sources/spinoza/ethics_elwes_1883.txt`. Kane and Pereboom are characterized by published positions; no fabricated quotations.

## Falsifiers (committed before arguments)

| Side | Falsifier (what would refute this side on its own terms) |
|---|---|
| **L** | A case, or a coherent account, of an agent who is responsible and free in the ordinary sense while every event, that choice included, is caused by the total prior state — such that "could have done otherwise given that state" is false — without the account collapsing into a change of subject. |
| **H** | A clear difference, checkable in behavior or in the agent's later control work, that knowledge of causes makes — a difference that is not already described as "more of the same causal chain" — so that calling the agent freer is not a mere relabeling. |
| **B** | Either (i) uncaused events at the scale of choices that still ground responsibility better than A5 + D-free, or (ii) a demonstration that Definition 15(b) (knowledge of causes) never changes how the agent acts or only restates prior causes, or (iii) a successful demand that "free" must mean could-have-done-otherwise on pain of equivocation. |

---

## Side L — Libertarian (Kane-style)

**L1 (the intuition).** Ordinary blame and praise assume that, at the moment of choice, the agent could have done otherwise. If the total prior state fixed the choice, saying "you should not have" is theater. Frankfurt-style cases (counterfactual interveners) do not erase the need for openness in the actual sequence; they only complicate one test for it (Kane's line against taking Frankfurt as settling the debate).

**L2 (undetermined but not mere chance).** Some self-forming actions are undetermined, yet not random: the agent's efforts and character tip a process that was not fixed in advance. Chance alone is not freedom (the book agrees: A5's note). The libertarian claim is that indeterminacy *inside* the agent's own trying can still be the agent's.

**L3 (P3 changes the subject).** D-free answers a different question — how much of what happened flowed through the agent's organization and knowledge — and then reuses the word "freedom." Compatibilist redefinitions have a long history (Hobbes, Hume, Spinoza). They can be useful psychology; they are not what the dispute was about.

**L4 (Spinoza as opponent, not authority).** E2P48 denies absolute or free will. That is a cost, not a proof. If the mind is determined to wish this or that by an infinite chain of causes, the first-person "mine" (A2(b)) is a description of a link in a chain, not a source that could have done otherwise.

**Conclusion of L.** Without could-have-done-otherwise (or an undetermined self-forming action that belongs to the agent), P3 is a theory of intelligent caused behavior, not of freedom.

---

## Side H — Hard incompatibilist (Pereboom-style)

**H1 (the source problem).** Even if the agent could not have done otherwise, what matters for basic desert is whether the agent was the *ultimate* source of the action. No finite agent is (E1P28; A5). Degrees of organization and insight do not create an ultimate source.

**H2 (relabeling).** Definition 15 says: more from the agent's organization, more knowledge of causes → freer. Translate out the word "freer" and what remains is: more of the agent's prior structure and information caused the outcome. That can be true and important for prediction, therapy, and education. It does not revive freedom; it renames causation-through-the-agent.

**H3 (knowledge does not break the chain).** E5P3 (a passion ceases when we form a clear idea of it) is a causal claim about ideas and affects. If it is true, clear ideas are further causes. Insight is not an escape hatch from A5; it is more A5. The turnspit that "knows" it is a turnspit is still a turnspit (pressing Kant's image harder than P3's Scholium 3 allows).

**H4 (forward-looking norms survive).** Blame as training, restraint, and repair can remain (verse 146†). What does not remain is basic desert. P3's honesty about ultimate responsibility is already halfway to H; it should finish the trip and drop the word "freedom."

**Conclusion of H.** A5 + D-free + P3 is a useful account of caused agency and learning. Calling it freedom is a relabeling that invites readers to hear desert where the book has already given it up.

---

## Side B — The book

**B1 (two senses, already split).** Strict freedom is E1D7 and belongs only to Nature (D-Nature, Scholium 4). Finite freedom is degree (D-free, Definition 15). L demands the strict sense (or an undetermined substitute) for finite agents; that demand is rejected as impossible for anything that takes energy from outside, not as unanswered.

**B2 (chance is not a third way).** If L's indeterminacy at choice scale is uncaused, A5 falls and what enters is chance, which L itself denies is enough (L2). If the indeterminacy is still caused by the agent's efforts, L has conceded B's pattern under another name. The collider should not let L hold both "undetermined" and "not chance" without a mechanism that is still the agent's *and* not a cause.

**B3 (knowledge is not idle).** Definition 15(b) predicts a checkable difference: agents who know the causes of what they do can update control (D-work) — drop a cause, strengthen one, or endorse a necessary one. That is not a new uncaused will; it is the control work using stored information. H2 must show that this never happens, or that every such update is "already in the prior state" in a way that makes the knowledge ascription false. The book's falsifier (ii) accepts that burden.

**B4 (first person without ghost).** On A2(b), a caused choice can still be *mine*: one event, two descriptions (verses 136–139). That reading is declared and unscored. It does not answer L's could-have-done-otherwise. It answers the fear that A5 erases the agent. H may still call "mine" cosmetic; B keeps it as the vantage from which knowledge of causes is had.

**B5 (desert vs degree).** Verse 146† already denies ultimate responsibility. P3 does not restore it. What P3 claims is comparative: freer / less free under necessity. Readers who hear basic desert in "free" are misreading D-free. The book would rather lose the word in a collision than smuggle desert back in.

**Conclusion of B.** P3 stands if A5 stands and if knowledge of causes can change control. It does not give L what L asks, and it does not claim what H says must be claimed for the word to be honest.
