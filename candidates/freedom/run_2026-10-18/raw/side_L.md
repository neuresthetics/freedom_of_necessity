L1 (source of the ordinary concept). Ordinary praise and blame presuppose that the agent could have done otherwise given the past; the consequence argument (van Inwagen, as characterized) shows that if the past and laws fix the act, the agent has no choice about it. On Kane's published view, some choices made under inner conflict are undetermined at the neural level, and the agent makes either outcome hers by effort; such acts are not chance because both outcomes are willed for reasons. A5 is a model axiom, not a result, and leaves this room open. [stands on: A5 "Limit"; A5 failure clause]
