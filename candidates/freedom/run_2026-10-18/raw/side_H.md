H1 (source). If every event is caused (A5) and no finite thing is its own cause (E1P28), then the agent's organization and knowledge are themselves products of factors beyond its control; on Pereboom's published view this rules out basic-desert responsibility. [stands on: A5; D-free "Ultimate responsibility"]
H2 (relabeling). "Freer by degree" ranks agents by how much of the chain runs through their internal structure. That is a fact about causal architecture; calling it freedom adds a word, not a property. [stands on: D-free Def 15(a)]
H3 (knowledge is more chain). Clear knowledge of a cause is itself an effect (schooling, temperament). When it changes action it does so as one more cause; P3 step 5 admits knowing a cause can leave the agent bound. [stands on: P3 step 5; E5P3 as further causation]
H4 (what survives). Forward-looking practices (protection, moral formation, reconciliation) survive without free will; nothing in P3 needs the word "free" to keep them. [stands on: P3 "How P3 fails" bullet 2]
Conclusion. Unless 15(b) earns a checkable difference not already described as more causal chain, P3 is a relabeling.
