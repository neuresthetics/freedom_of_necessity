### Falsifier L (committed first)
A case, or a coherent account, of an agent who is responsible and free in the ordinary sense while every event, that choice included, is caused by the total prior state — such that "could have done otherwise given that state" is false — without the account collapsing into a change of subject.

### Falsifier H (committed second)
A clear difference, checkable in behavior or in the agent's later control work, that knowledge of causes makes — a difference that is not already described as "more of the same causal chain" — so that calling the agent freer is not a mere relabeling.

### Falsifier B (committed third)
Either (i) uncaused events at the scale of choices that still ground responsibility better than A5 + D-free, or (ii) a demonstration that Definition 15(b) (knowledge of causes) never changes how the agent acts or only restates prior causes, or (iii) a successful demand that "free" must mean could-have-done-otherwise on pain of equivocation.
