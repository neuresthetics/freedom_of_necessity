**Mind under the aspect of eternity** *(proposition, id: P16)*.

**Why this follows.** Part V eternity-of-mind ground, kept thin: third-kind knowledge (*Intuition*, D-intuition; P8) under *Eternity* (D-eternity), for the mind of A2, with Nature as substance (A6).

**Proposition 16 (candidate).** Insofar as the mind (*A human body, and its mind*, A2) knows things by the third kind of knowledge (*Intuition*, D-intuition; P8), it is conceived under a *species of eternity* (*Eternity*, D-eternity) — pattern of E5P22–P23.

**Stated sense.** Candidate, not a scored immortality doctrine. Uses A2(a) for the mind-as-organization subject; A2(b) first-person identity remains declared.

**What this does not say.** It does not say a person lasts forever in duration after death. Duration and eternity are different (D-eternity). Personal survival as indefinite lasting is not claimed.

**How P16 fails.** If P8's third-kind rise is idle, or if eternity collapses into indefinite duration.

**Cap.** Cites A2, P8, and A6. Effective confidence = min(A2, P8, A6) once scored. Definitions add nothing.

- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E5P22; E5P23; E5P29.
