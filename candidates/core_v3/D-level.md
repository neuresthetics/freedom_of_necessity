**Levels of organization** *(definition, id: D-level)*.

**Why this follows.** P1 uses levels; give expansion room for many layers.

**Definition 64 (level).** A *level* is a grain of description at which something can be graded as a whole (*Whole...*, D-whole) and can stand as a member of a whole at a coarser grain. Examples in this chain: cell to multicellular body to household/workplace to society/state. The stack of levels stops short of *Nature taken as one* (D-Nature): in Nature is not member of Nature.

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
