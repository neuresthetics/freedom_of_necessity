**Why this follows.** Volume coverage of E3 list without fifty micro-files.

**Definition 52 (further named affects).** The remaining names in Spinoza list — including devotion, gratitude, indignation, ambition, repentance, self-approval, emulation, daring, timidity, courtesy, vengeance, cruelty, jealousy, and related compounds — are read in this chain as compounds of *Joy* (D-joy), *Sorrow* (D-sorrow), *Desire* (D-desire), *Love* (D-love), and *Hate* (D-hate), following E3 Definitions of the Emotions.

**Scoring.** This catalog is not separately collided. Bondage and blessedness (P4, P5) still ride on degree-freedom, not on counting names.
