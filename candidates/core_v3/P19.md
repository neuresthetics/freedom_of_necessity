**Degree of whole tracks degree of freedom only loosely** *(proposition, id: P19)*.

**Why this follows.** Prevent collapse of two degree talks as the chain grows.

**Proposition 19.** The degree of being a whole (*Whole...*, D-whole; *A whole at one level, a part at the next*, P1) and the degree of freedom (*Freedom, strict and by degree*, D-free; P3) are not the same scale. An organization can perform the works strongly and still act mostly from unclear outside causes. Freedom tracks own-organization plus knowledge of causes; whole-degree tracks the works lowest grade.

**Cap.** min(P1, P3).
