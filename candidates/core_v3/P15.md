**Civic peace rises with common notions of utility** *(proposition, id: P15)*.

**Why this follows.** *Common notions* (D-common-notion) under reason (P8) meet politics at shared utility under law.

**Proposition 15.** Civic peace rises as members of the state (A8) share *common notions* (*Common notions*, D-common-notion) of what is *useful* (*Useful*, D-useful) under *Law as common decree* (D-law), which is the political face of rising knowledge (P8) in service of the state end (P14).

**Stated sense.** Comparative peace claim. Does not claim that law alone produces reason, or that dissent is never useful.

**How P15 fails.** If P8 or P14 fails, or if civic peace is shown never to track shared notions of utility.
