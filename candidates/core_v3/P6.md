**Finite organizations are modes of Nature** *(proposition, id: P6)*.

**Why this follows.** With A6 and D-mode, the body (A2) and self-producing wholes (A3) take their Part I place.

**Proposition 6.** Every finite organization graded in this chain — including the body named in *A human body, and its mind* (A2) and the self-producing things sorted by A3 — is a *mode* of Nature (*What a mode is*, D-mode; *Nature taken as one*, D-Nature), not a substance (*Only Nature answers to substance*, A6). Being in Nature is not being a member of Nature (D-Nature).

**Demonstration (sketch).**
1. By A6, only Nature is substance.
2. By D-mode, what is in another and conceived through another is a mode.
3. A2 body and A3 finite self-producers exist among everything that exists (D-Nature) and are limited by other things of the same kind (D-whole domain). So they are modes.

**Cap.** min(A2, A3, A6).
