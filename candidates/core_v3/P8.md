**Clear knowledge of causes rises through the kinds** *(proposition, id: P8)*.

**Why this follows.** Connect the knowledge ladder to degree-freedom.

**Proposition 8.** Other things equal, the agent named in *A human body, and its mind* (A2) is freer in the degree sense (P3; D-free Definition 15) as its knowledge of the causes of what it does rises from the first kind toward the second and third (*Three kinds of knowledge*, D-knowledge).

**Cap.** min(A2, P3). Definitions add nothing.

**References.**
- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E2P40 Scholium 2; E5P3 as background report.
