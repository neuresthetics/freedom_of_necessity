**Inadequate idea** *(definition, id: D-inadequate-idea)*.

**Why this follows.** Pair with adequate idea.

**Definition 37 (inadequate idea).** An *inadequate* or *confused* idea is one that is mutilated and incomplete — typically when the mind knows things only through bodily affections without the chain of causes (E2P29; E2P35 pattern).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
