## V001 · `D-I` — What "I" names

"I" is the name this thought uses for its thinker —
or, if no thinker stands apart, for the thinking itself.

Like saying "it rains" for the raining:
the word need not smuggle in a soul.


---

## V002 · `D-exist` — What exists

A doing exists while it is being done.
To last is only to keep being done.

No more metaphysics in the word than that.


---

## V003 · `A1` — I exist

I exist.

Doubt it, and the doubting is already here —
a lamp you cannot switch off by staring at it.

What I am, how long, for whom: not yet.
Only this: the thinking goes on.


---

## V004 · `A2` — A human body, and its mind

I am this human body of cells,
and what cells build around themselves.

Close these eyes — the seeing ends.
Hurt one place — a power goes with it.

I read mind as this same body
told from the inside.
That reading is a choice of story, not a proof.
Why it feels like anything at all stays open.


---

## V005 · `D-work` — Work, and the seven works

A work is a loop that keeps a living pattern going,
done by parts the pattern itself remakes.

The heart pumps; the body builds the heart.
A charger plugged in by other hands is not the robot's work.

Seven such loops we name by what they do —
edge, feed, clear, change, mend, signal, remember —
and ask, taking the pattern apart, which ones were truly its own.


---

## V006 · `D-whole` — Whole, degree, member, level, self-production, sameness

How whole is a thing? Ask its weakest work.
A storm may roar and still remember nothing.

Above nothing, there are only degrees —
shared fate, a part that serves, levels stacked like bowls.
Wholeness is not the same question as how sure you are.


---

## V007 · `A3` — The seven works sort things the way self-production does

What remakes itself tends to keep all seven loops as one.
What does not, drops at least one.

A sorting rule, not a sermon —
and open to hard cases at the edge.


---

## V008 · `A4` — The society I live in is a whole to a degree

The society I live in holds together to a degree,
work by work: its edges, its feeds, its signals, its memory.

I am one of its members —
my life runs through its loops; I help at least one turn.
That it feels, as I feel: not claimed.


---

## V009 · `P1` — A whole at one level, a part at the next

I am a whole in my own skin,
and a part in the city that holds me.

Both true, like a brick that is wall and is clay —
level by level, no need for a highest floor.


---

## V010 · `P2` — A collective mind happens

A people can steer as one,
pass signals, keep a memory —
a mind as a whirlpool is a mind:
a happening, not a ghost with a face.

That it feels with one feeling: not said.


---

## V011 · `A5` — Every event is caused

Nothing happens from nowhere —
not a choice, not a flinch, not a thought.

If chance steals the steering wheel,
that is not freedom either;
it is weather inside the skull.


---

## V012 · `D-free` — Freedom, strict and by degree

Strict freedom belongs to what needs no outside —
Nature alone, if anything.

For us: freer by degrees
when what we do grows from what we are
and from seeing the causes clear —
even the ones that hurt to see.


---

## V013 · `P3` — Freedom by degrees under necessity

*Freedom doesn't need that you could have done otherwise,
given all that came before.
It needs that what you did came from you.*

*To understand a cause is to know where it came from:
genes, schooling, or a meddler's hand.
A cause seen at its source can change:
sometimes it loosens, sometimes it steadies into resolve.
And sometimes you see it whole and are still bound,
because it is necessary.
Freedom is not escaping the cause.
It is knowing it, origin and all,
and acting from that knowledge.*

— Jason (preserved from the verse companion)


---

## V014 · `D-affect` — Affect, and change of power

An affect is a change in your power to act —
the works rising or falling —
and the idea of that change riding with it.

Joy names a rise; sadness a fall;
desire is the lean that keeps you going.


---

## V015 · `D-bondage` — Bondage

Bondage is being moved from outside,
or by storms whose weather you cannot name.

Same ladder as freedom —
only downward.


---

## V016 · `D-blessedness` — Blessedness

Blessedness is not a medal after the race.
It is the strength in the running
as seeing grows and the works run clearer.


---

## V017 · `P4` — Bondage is acting from what is outside

You are bound insofar as something else steers,
or a feeling drives you while its cause stays fog.

Clear the fog, reclaim the steering —
bondage lessens by degrees.


---

## V018 · `P5` — Blessedness is the power itself

The prize is not waiting in another life.
The prize is the power itself
as freedom rises in this one.


---

## V019 · `P19` — Degree of whole tracks degree of freedom only loosely

A tight circle can still be a cage.
A loose one can still be free.

How whole, and how free:
two meters, not one.


---

## V020 · `P21` — Society can be freer without feeling

A city may grow wiser in its laws and signals
without ever feeling as one creature feels.

Freer together — maybe —
without a corporate soul.
(A hope with a label: conjecture.)
