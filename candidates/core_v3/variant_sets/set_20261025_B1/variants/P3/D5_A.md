**Freedom by degrees under necessity** *(proposition, id: P3)*.

**BASE (locked).** Seed-spine of core v3. Snapshot: [BASE.md](BASE.md). Grow from this; do not rewrite the base without author reopen.

**Why this follows A5 and D-free.** *Every event is caused* (A5) and *Freedom, strict and by degree* (D-free) give necessity and both senses of freedom. This proposition derives the book's working claim: degree-freedom is compatible with A5, and does not require could-have-done-otherwise.

**Proposition 3.** Freedom, in the book's degree sense (D-free, Definition 15), is compatible with every event being caused (A5). The body this thinking goes with (A1, A2 as scored) is freer to the degree that what it does follows from its own organization and from clear knowledge of the causes of what it does. Freedom does not require that the agent could have done otherwise, given all that came before.

**Demonstration.**

1. This thinking is going on now (A1). By A2, clause (a), it goes with a human body made of cells: that body's organization is how its components are arranged and act on one another.
2. Every event is caused, a choice included (A5). So any choice this body makes has a cause. An uncaused choice is ruled out by A5; if A5 falls, what remains at that scale is chance, which is not freedom (A5, what would lower it; D-free).
3. By D-free, Definition 14, only Nature is free in the strict sense. This body takes energy from outside (D-work, energy; [parked D-Nature](PARKED_VOLUME/D-Nature.md)), so it is constrained in E1D7's word. Strict freedom is not available to it.
4. By D-free, Definition 15, a finite agent is freer to the degree that (a) what it does follows from its own organization and (b) it knows the causes of what it does clearly. Both clauses are comparative and can rise while every event remains caused (step 2). So degree-freedom does not require an uncaused will, and does not require that, given all that came before, the agent could have done otherwise.
5. Control is the work of combining signals and stored information into coordinated change (D-work, Definition 6). When this body performs control as one, the coordination is carried by structures its own organization produces or maintains (D-work, Definitions 3 and 5). That is clause (a) of Definition 15 in the body's own works: what it does follows from what it is, as far as that combining goes. Clause (b) rises when the causes fed into that combining are known clearly — including causes that began outside the body (schooling, a meddler's hand) and causes that are necessary features of its organization. Knowing a cause does not always loosen it; it can steady into resolve, or leave the agent bound because the cause is necessary. In all three cases the act can still be freer in Definition 15's sense than the same act done in ignorance of its cause.
6. Therefore freedom in Definition 15's sense is compatible with A5, and this body is freer by degree as steps 4–5 say. On A2's declared reading (clause (b)), on which I am this body and the mind is that organization described in the first person, the same holds of me, and a caused choice can still be *mine* (Scholium 1). ∎

**Scholium 1 (what "mine" claims).** Step 6's scored reading says: the body this thinking goes with acts more or less from its own organization and from knowledge of causes. That is all the demonstration needs for compatibility and degree. The further claim that a caused choice is still *mine* in the first person — "neither outranks the other" with the third-person causal description — uses A2's declared reading (one organization, two descriptions). That reading is unscored and adds nothing. What would lower the thesis that rides on it is a choice with no first-person side, or an exclusion argument that the first-person description is never causally the right level (verses 136–137).

**Scholium 2 (Spinoza, reports).** E2P48 denies absolute or free will in the mind (A5 Scholium). E3D1–D2 give the act/passive pattern Definition 15 follows. E4P66 Note calls the reason-led person a free man. E5P3: a passion ceases to be a passion when we form a clear and distinct idea of it. E5P42: blessedness is not the reward of virtue, but virtue itself. None of these reports raise P3's confidence.

**Scholium 3 (Kant and the turnspit).** Kant objected that freedom which is only a cause in the agent at work is the freedom of a turnspit (*Critique of Practical Reason*, Ak. 5:96–97). P3's reply is Definition 15(b): a turnspit does not know what moves it. The reply is the book's thesis about knowledge, not a finding that Kant is answered. It is stated here so the collider can attack it.

**How P3 fails.**

- If A5 fails (uncaused events at the scale of choices), the compatibility claim is moot: the rival is chance, not libertarian freedom.
- If degree-freedom collapses into a mere relabeling of "caused by the agent's prior states," with knowledge adding nothing that can change how the agent acts, then Definition 15(b) is idle and P3's distinctive claim fails (hard-incompatibilist side of the collider).
- If freedom requires could-have-done-otherwise given the total prior state, then Definition 15 is the wrong definition and P3 does not answer the libertarian demand (libertarian side of the collider).
