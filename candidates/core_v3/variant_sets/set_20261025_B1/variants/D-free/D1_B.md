**Freedom, strict and by degree** *(definition, id: D-free)*.

**BASE (locked).** Seed-spine of core v3. Snapshot: [BASE.md](BASE.md). Grow from this; do not rewrite the base without author reopen.

**Why this follows A5.** *Every event is caused* (A5) rules out uncaused will. Nature taken as one ([parked D-Nature](PARKED_VOLUME/D-Nature.md)) already foreshadows that only Nature is free in Spinoza's strict sense (E1D7). This definition fixes both senses the book uses: strict (Nature alone) and by degree (finite agents). It adds no confidence.

**Nature (inline).** *Nature taken as one* means everything that exists, taken together, with nothing besides it. Full candidate text is parked at [PARKED_VOLUME/D-Nature.md](PARKED_VOLUME/D-Nature.md). Only that whole meets the strict sense of freedom (E1D7).

**Definition 14 (free, strict sense).** Strict *freedom* (E1D7): existence solely from the necessity of one's own nature, action determined by oneself alone. On the other hand, a thing is *necessary, or rather constrained*, when it is determined by something external to itself to a fixed and definite method of existence or action (E1D7). Only Nature taken as one meets the strict sense (parked D-Nature, Scholium 4; E1P17 Corollary 2). Every whole D-whole grades takes energy from other things (D-work, energy), so each is constrained in E1D7's word.

**Definition 15 (free, by degree).** A finite agent is *freer* to the degree that (a) what it does follows from its own organization — the structures and constraints its components impose on one another (A2; D-work, Definition 3) — rather than from causes that merely push it from outside, and (b) it knows those causes clearly, so that what it does can be understood through what it is. The degree is comparative, not a second scale beside D-whole's grades. Zero on (a) or (b) is not a failure of being a whole; it is a low degree of freedom.

**What the words borrow.**

- *Organization.* Same sense as A2 and D-work, Definition 3: how components are arranged and act on one another. Control (D-work, Definition 6) is the work that combines signals and stored information into coordinated change; when that combining is done as one by the agent, and the agent knows the causes at work, clause (a) and clause (b) rise together.
- *Adequate cause (report, not a new definition).* Spinoza: "By an adequate cause, I mean a cause through which its effect can be clearly and distinctly perceived" (E3D1). "I say that we act when anything takes place, either within us or externally to us, whereof we are the adequate cause... On the other hand, I say that we are passive as regards something when... we being only the partial cause" (E3D2). Definition 15 uses that pair as the pattern for degree: more from what the agent is, less as partial cause. It does not re-define "adequate."
- *Knowledge of causes.* Clear knowledge that a cause is at work, including where it came from (genes, schooling, a meddler's hand, or a necessary feature of the agent's own organization). Knowing a cause does not always loosen it; sometimes it steadies into resolve, and sometimes the agent sees it whole and is still bound (verse 144's thesis, to be carried by P3 if promoted).

**What D-free leaves out.**

- *Could-have-done-otherwise.* Neither sense requires that, given all that came before, the agent could have done otherwise. That demand belongs to the libertarian side of the collider, not to this definition.
- *Feeling.* D-free does not say why anything is felt. A2's limit stays open.
- *Ultimate responsibility.* No finite thing is its own cause (E1P28; A5). Forward-looking responsibility is a norm (verse 146†), not part of the definition.
- *Society's freedom.* Verse 151‡ extends the degree sense to A4's society as a conjecture; D-free does not settle it.

**Scholium (the free man, report).** E4P66 Note distinguishes a person led solely by emotion or opinion from one led by reason: "I call the former a slave, and the latter a free man." E5P42 closes the *Ethics*: "Blessedness is not the reward of virtue, but virtue itself." These are reports. They sketch where the degree sense points; they do not score D-free.

**Limit.** This is a definition. It fixes what "free" means in the book. It does not make P3 or any claim more likely.
