## V012 · `D-free` — Freedom, strict and by degree

**Kind:** definition · **Confidence:** (definition; unscored) · **Cap:** definition; adds nothing
**Rests on:** `D-I`, `D-exist`, `D-work`, `A5`
**Supports (sample):** `P3`, `D-affect`, `D-bondage`, `D-blessedness`, `P4`, `P5`, `P19`, `P21`

**Claim (entry).** Strict freedom: from own nature alone (Nature). Degree-freedom: from own organization + clear knowledge of causes.

**Dependency analysis.** Definition. Rests on D-I, D-exist, D-work, A5. Supports P3, D-bondage, D-blessedness, D-strict-free, D-clear-knowledge. Adds no confidence. Could-have-done-otherwise is not part of either definition.

**Verse.**
Two senses, kept apart. Strict (E1D7 pattern): only what acts from its own nature alone — in this chain, Nature. Finite agents take energy from outside, so strict freedom is not available to them. Degree: freer as action follows from own organization and from clear knowledge of causes (including origins). Knowledge can loosen, steady, or leave you bound by necessity — all three can still be freer than the same act in ignorance.

**References.** Spinoza E1D7; E3D1–D2 pattern; E4P66 Note; E5P3.
