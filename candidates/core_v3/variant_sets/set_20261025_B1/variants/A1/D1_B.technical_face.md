**Claim (entry).** I exist.

**Dependency analysis.** Sole scored root. Rests on D-I, D-exist (terms). Cap **1.0; caps nothing**. Supports A2 (subject), P3, and expansion that needs a first-person subject (P-sleep-and-A1, P-digital-A1, …). Denial by the thinker proves the claim (doubting is thinking going on).

**Verse.**
A1: I exist. Not by definition — by the impossibility of false thought of it. To doubt that I exist is to be doubting; the doubting is going on; by D-exist that activity exists; by D-I "I" may name no more than that thinking. Certainty is first-personal and holds while the thinking goes on. What I am, how long, and for whom are not in A1. A digital intelligence can produce the sentence; whether it is certain *for it* stays open (A1 Scholium 1).
