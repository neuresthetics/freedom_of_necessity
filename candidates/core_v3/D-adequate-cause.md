**Adequate and partial cause** *(definition, id: D-adequate-cause)*.

**Why this follows.** Degree-freedom (D-free) already leans on this pair; give it its own entry.

**Definition 25 (adequate cause).** An *adequate cause* is a cause through which its effect can be clearly and distinctly perceived. A *partial* or *inadequate cause* is one through which the effect cannot be perceived alone (E3D1).

**Definition 26 (acting / being passive).** We *act* when something happens of which we are the adequate cause; we are *passive* when we are only a partial cause (E3D2).

**Use.** *Freedom, strict and by degree* (D-free) and *Affect...* (D-affect) already use this pattern. This entry only fixes the words.

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
