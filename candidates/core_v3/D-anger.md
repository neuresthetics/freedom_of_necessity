**Anger** *(definition, id: D-anger)*.

**Why this follows.** Hostile affect.

**Definition 50 (anger).** *Anger* is the desire whereby we are urged through hatred to injure one whom we hate (E3 Definitions of the Emotions, 36).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.

**References.**
- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E3 Definitions of the Emotions, 36.
