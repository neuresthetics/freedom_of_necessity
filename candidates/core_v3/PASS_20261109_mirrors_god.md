## Rules applied

1. **God / borrowed-word rule** (from the scholium on *Nature taken as one*, D-Nature-one, point 3). No entry or verse may lean on "God", or on a God-adjacent word, for anything "the one order of causes" (or the chain's own working sense of the word) would not also support. Words checked: God, divine, sacred, holy, blessed/blessedness, reverence, worship, love of God, eternity/eternal, purpose, worth/worthy, value, virtue, soul, spirit, grace, salvation, faith, piety, prayer, providence, destiny/fate, miracle, creation/design, heaven, sin, redemption, cosmic, ultimate, infinite, absolute, perfection, transcendent, mystic. Test: substitute the plain phrase; if the claim changes, the entry is borrowing.
2. **Mirrors** (author's direction, 2026-11-09). The thesis is one event, two descriptions: from outside (third person: body, causes, works) and from inside (first person). Where that reading genuinely applies, each entry gets a short **Mirror (outside / inside)** block whose two lines match clause for clause. The mirror is part of A2's declared reading (b): **unscored, adds no confidence, changes no cap**. Frontmatter, cites, and caps were not touched. Entries where the reading does not fit are listed N/A with a reason; it was not forced.
   - Entry files: block inserted before *Failure / limit* (expansion) or before *References* (BASE).
   - Technical track: the same outside / inside pair, labelled *A2(b) reading, unscored*.
   - Poetic track: a matching couplet, "Seen from without: … / Seen from within: …".
