**Eternity** *(definition, id: D-eternity)*.

**Why this follows.** Part V ground needs eternity != lasting forever in time.

**Definition 62 (eternity).** *Eternity* is existence itself, insofar as it is conceived necessarily to follow solely from the definition of an eternal thing (E1D8). Eternity is not indefinite duration (*What exists*, D-exist lasting); duration is existence continued.

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.

- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E1D8; E2D5.
