**Signs and hearsay** *(definition, id: D-sign)*.

**Why this follows.** Complete first-kind sources.

**Definition 66 (signs).** Knowledge from *signs* — words, testimony, reading — belongs to the first kind of knowledge until the causes are ordered by reason or intuition (E2P40 Scholium 2).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.

**References.**
- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E2P40 Scholium 2.
