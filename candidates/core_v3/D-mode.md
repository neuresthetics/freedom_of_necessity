**What a mode is** *(definition, id: D-mode)*.

**Why this follows.** With substance and attribute named, finite things need their Part I name: mode.

**Definition 22 (mode).** A *mode* is an affection of substance; that which is in another, through which also it is conceived (E1D5).

**Working sense.** Every finite whole graded by *Whole...* (D-whole) is a mode in this sense: it exists in Nature (*Nature taken as one*, D-Nature) and is conceived through relations that are not itself alone. Being a mode is not the same as being a *member* of a whole (D-whole).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
