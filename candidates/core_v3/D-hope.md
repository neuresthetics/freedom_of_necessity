**Hope** *(definition, id: D-hope)*.

**Why this follows.** Future-directed affect.

**Definition 44 (hope).** *Hope* is an inconstant joy arisen from the image of a thing future or past, of whose outcome we are in doubt (E3 Definitions of the Emotions, 12).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.

**References.**
- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E3 Definitions of the Emotions, 12.
