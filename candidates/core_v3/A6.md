**Only Nature answers to substance** *(axiom, model, id: A6)*.

**Why this follows.** *What substance means* (D-substance) and *Nature taken as one* (D-Nature) are fixed. Uniqueness — that only Nature answers to substance — needs an axiom slot; a full E1P14 demonstration is not claimed.

**Axiom.** Only *Nature taken as one* (D-Nature) answers to *What substance means* (D-substance) in this chain.

**Stated sense.** No finite whole (D-whole) is a substance. There is not a second substance beside Nature. This does not prove Spinoza full Part I sequence; it states the book working commitment.

**What would lower it.** A coherent finite thing that is in itself and conceived through itself without Nature.
