**The free person is led by reason more than by opinion** *(proposition, id: P13)*.

**Why this follows.** Spinoza's free-man report (E4P66 Note) becomes a live proposition once P3, P8, and P12 are on the chain.

**Proposition 13.** A person is freer in the degree sense (D-free Definition 15; P3) to the degree they are led by reason rather than by opinion or imagination alone (*Three kinds of knowledge*, D-knowledge; P8), which is the same as rising virtue (P12). Pattern of E4P66 Note.

**Stated sense.** Comparative. Does not claim the free person feels nothing, or never uses imagination.

**How P13 fails.** If P3/P8/P12 fail, or if "free person" is redefined as uncaused will.

**Cap.** Cites P3, P8, and P12. Effective confidence = min(P3, P8, P12) once scored. Definitions add nothing.
