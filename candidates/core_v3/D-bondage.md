**Bondage** *(definition, id: D-bondage)*.

**Why this follows.** Give P4 key word a definition home.

**Definition 71 (bondage).** *Bondage* (servitude) is the condition in which what an agent does follows from outside causes or from affects whose causes are unclear — the inverse of degree-freedom (D-free Definition 15; *Bondage is acting from what is outside*, P4; E4 Pref. pattern).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.

- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E4 Preface.
