**Bondage** *(definition, id: D-bondage)*.

**BASE (locked).** Seed-spine of core v3. Snapshot: [BASE.md](BASE.md). Grow from this; do not rewrite the base without author reopen.

**Why this follows.** The inverse of degree-freedom needs a word of its own before the proposition that derives it (*Bondage is acting from what is outside*, P4).

**Definition 71 (bondage).** *Bondage* (servitude) is the condition in which what an agent does follows from outside causes or from affects whose causes are unclear — the inverse of degree-freedom (*Freedom, strict and by degree*, D-free, Definition 15; E4 Pref. pattern).

**Order.** This definition fixes the word. P4 derives the degree claim under A5 and P3; it does not redefine bondage.

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.

**Mirror (outside / inside).** One event, two descriptions; part of A2's declared reading (b).
- *Outside:* What the agent does follows from causes outside it, or from changes in it whose causes it does not track.
- *Inside:* I am pushed, or I am driven by something I cannot name.

- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E4 Preface.
