**Virtue is acting from one's own nature under reason** *(proposition, id: P12)*.

**Why this follows.** *Virtue as power* (D-virtue) names virtue as power of acting. Under P3 and P8, that power rises with reason.

**Proposition 12.** For the agent of *A human body, and its mind* (A2), *virtue* (*Virtue as power*, D-virtue) is power of acting from its own organization under reason (*Reason*, D-reason; *Clear knowledge of causes rises through the kinds*, P8), which is the same rise tracked by *Freedom by degrees under necessity* (P3).

**Stated sense.** Working sense of E4D8 / E4P24 in this chain's language. Not a list of virtues; one degree claim.

**How P12 fails.** If P3 or P8 fails, or if virtue is stipulated as a supernatural prize unrelated to power of acting.

**Cap.** Cites A2, P3, and P8. Effective confidence = min(A2, P3, P8) once scored. Definitions add nothing.

**References.**
- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E4D8; E4P24.
