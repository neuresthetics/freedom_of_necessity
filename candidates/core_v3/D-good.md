**Good** *(definition, id: D-good)*.

**Why this follows.** Part IV ground after affects.

**Definition 53 (good).** By *good* I mean that which we certainly know to be useful to us (E4D1). In this chain, useful means increasing or aiding power of acting (*Affect, and change of power*, D-affect) while we strive (*Striving to persist*, D-strive).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence. Not a cosmic value outside striving beings.

- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E4D1.
