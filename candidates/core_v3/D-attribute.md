**What an attribute is** *(definition, id: D-attribute)*.

**Why this follows.** Substance (D-substance) needs the word for how substance is conceived: attribute.

**Definition 21 (attribute).** An *attribute* is that which the intellect perceives of substance as constituting its essence (E1D4).

**The two we know (report).** Thought and Extension are attributes of God/Nature (E2P1; E2P2). *A human body, and its mind* (A2) already uses the one-thing-two-ways pattern (E2P7 Scholium). This definition does not add a third attribute and does not score how many there are.

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
