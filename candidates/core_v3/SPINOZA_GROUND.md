## Method of extension

1. Keep the seed and the seven-work / degree apparatus.
2. Pull necessity and freedom (A5 → D-free → P3) before affects.
3. Restate conatus and affect in work-language (D-strive, D-affect).
4. Derive bondage and blessedness as degrees under P3 (P4, P5).
5. Leave substance metaphysics and the third kind of knowledge as reports or declared readings until the author asks for scored entries.
