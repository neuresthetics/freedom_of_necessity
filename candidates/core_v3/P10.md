**Active affects follow from own organization** *(proposition, id: P10)*.

**Why this follows.** Turn D-affect active/passive report into a proposition.

**Proposition 10.** An affect in the agent of *A human body, and its mind* (A2) is *active* to the degree that the change in power of acting (*Affect...*, D-affect) follows from the agent own organization as adequate cause (*Adequate and partial cause*, D-adequate-cause), under *Freedom by degrees under necessity* (P3).

**Cap.** min(A2, P3).
