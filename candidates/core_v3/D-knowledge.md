**Three kinds of knowledge** *(definition, id: D-knowledge)*.

**Why this follows.** P3 clear knowledge of causes needs the ladder named.

**Definition 35 (three kinds of knowledge).** Knowledge is of three kinds (E2P40 Scholium 2):
1. *Opinion* or *imagination* — from singular things through the senses, from signs, or from undirected experience.
2. *Reason* — from common notions and adequate ideas of properties.
3. *Intuition* — as in *Intuition* (D-intuition).

**Use in this chain.** Degree-freedom (D-free; P3) rises with clearer knowledge of causes; kinds (2) and (3) are the working ladder for that clarity.

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
