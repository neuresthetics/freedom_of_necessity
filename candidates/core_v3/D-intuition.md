**Intuition** *(definition, id: D-intuition)*.

**Why this follows.** Third rung; used later by eternity and blessedness reports.

**Definition 34 (intuition).** *Intuition* is knowledge of the third kind: a kind of knowing that proceeds from an adequate idea of the formal essence of certain attributes of God (Nature) to adequate knowledge of the essence of things (E2P40 Scholium 2).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence. Full blessedness-as-amor-Dei remains declared report in P5.

**References.**
- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E2P40 Scholium 2.
