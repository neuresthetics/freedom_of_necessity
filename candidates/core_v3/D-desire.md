**Desire** *(definition, id: D-desire)*.

**Why this follows.** Third primary affect.

**Definition 41 (desire).** *Desire* is appetite with consciousness of the appetite; appetite is the striving itself (*Striving to persist*, D-strive) insofar as it relates to mind and body together (E3P9 Scholium pattern).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.

- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E3P9 Scholium.
