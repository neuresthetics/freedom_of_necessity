**Fear** *(definition, id: D-fear)*.

**Why this follows.** Pair with hope.

**Definition 45 (fear).** *Fear* is an inconstant sorrow arisen from the image of a thing future or past, of whose outcome we are in doubt (E3 Definitions of the Emotions, 13).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.

**References.**
- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E3 Definitions of the Emotions, 13.
