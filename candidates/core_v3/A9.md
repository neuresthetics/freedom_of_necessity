**Nothing comes from nothing in the finite order** *(axiom, model, id: A9)*.

**Why this follows.** *Every event is caused* (A5) rules out uncaused events. This axiom states the companion claim for lasting organizations: changes in their works have prior finite conditions.

**Axiom.** In the finite order, a change in an organization's works has prior finite conditions; nothing in that order pops into existence without antecedents (*Every event is caused*, A5; *Finite causes form an infinite chain*, A7).

**Stated sense.** Companion to A5, scoped to changes of organization under D-work / D-whole. Not a claim about vacuum physics or creation ex nihilo outside the finite order. The collider may later merge this with A5 or A7 if they prove the same claim.

**What would lower it.** A lasting finite organization whose works change with no prior finite conditions at all.

**Cap.** Cites A5. Effective confidence = min(own score, A5) once scored. A7 is named as pattern, not a cite (same finite-order chain).
