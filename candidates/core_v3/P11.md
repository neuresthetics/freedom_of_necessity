**Passive affects bind when causes stay unclear** *(proposition, id: P11)*.

**Why this follows.** Bridge affect taxonomy to bondage.

**Proposition 11.** Passive affects bind the agent — that is, raise *Bondage is acting from what is outside* (P4) — to the degree their causes are known only inadequately (*Inadequate idea*, D-inadequate-idea) rather than through the agent own organization (P3).

**Cap.** min(A2, P3, P4).
