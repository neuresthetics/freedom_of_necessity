**Natural right as power** *(definition, id: D-right)*.

**Why this follows.** Political ground from conatus.

**Definition 58 (natural right).** The *natural right* of each thing extends as far as its power extends: what each does by the laws of its own nature, it does by sovereign natural right (pattern of TTP ch. 16; TP 2; E4P37 Scholium 2).

**Link.** *Striving to persist* (D-strive) is the continuing of the works; right names that power without yet naming civil law.

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence. Not a moral permission slip for harm; later civil entries constrain.

**References.**
- Spinoza, B. *Theological-Political Treatise* ch. 16; *Political Treatise* 2; *Ethics* E4P37 Scholium 2.
