**Common notions** *(definition, id: D-common-notion)*.

**Why this follows.** Reason content.

**Definition 38 (common notions).** *Common notions* are ideas of those things which are common to all, and which are equally in the part and in the whole; they can only be conceived adequately (E2P37-P38; E2P40).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
