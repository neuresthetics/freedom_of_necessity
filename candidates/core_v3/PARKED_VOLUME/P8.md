**Clear knowledge of causes rises through the kinds** *(proposition, id: P8)*.

**Why this follows.** *Freedom by degrees under necessity* (P3) already ties freer to clear knowledge of causes. *Three kinds of knowledge* (D-knowledge) names the ladder; this proposition connects them.

**Proposition 8.** Other things equal, the agent named in *A human body, and its mind* (A2) is freer in the degree sense (P3; D-free Definition 15) as its knowledge of the causes of what it does rises from the first kind toward the second and third (*Three kinds of knowledge*, D-knowledge).

**Stated sense.** Comparative under P3 clause (b). Does not claim that intuition is common, or that the first kind never aids action. Uses A2(a) only; first-person identity reading unscored.

**How P8 fails.** If knowledge of causes never changes how the agent acts (P3 hard-incompatibilist failure), the ladder adds nothing.

**Cap.** Cites A2 and P3. Effective confidence = min(A2, P3) once scored. Definitions add nothing.
