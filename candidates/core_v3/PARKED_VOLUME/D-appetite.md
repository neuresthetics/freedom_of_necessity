**Appetite** *(definition, id: D-appetite)*.

**Why this follows.** Split desire partner term.

**Definition 69 (appetite).** *Appetite* is the striving to persist (*Striving to persist*, D-strive) insofar as it relates to mind and body together; *Desire* (D-desire) is appetite with consciousness of it (E3P9 Scholium).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.

**References.**
- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E3P9 Scholium.
