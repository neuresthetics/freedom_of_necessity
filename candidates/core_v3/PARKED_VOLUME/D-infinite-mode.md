**Infinite modes** *(definition, id: D-infinite-mode)*.

**Why this follows.** Modes include infinite ones in the Ethics; the chain names them so later talk has a slot.

**Definition 23 (infinite mode).** An *infinite mode* is an affection that follows from the absolute nature of some attribute of Nature, either immediately or by mediation of another infinite modification (E1P21-P23 pattern).

**Report only.** Motion-and-rest (Extension) and infinite intellect (Thought) are usual examples; this chain does not score which lists are complete.

**Limit.** Definition / report. Adds no confidence.

- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E1P21; E1P22; E1P23.
