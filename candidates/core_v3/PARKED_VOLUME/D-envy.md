**Envy** *(definition, id: D-envy)*.

**Why this follows.** Social affect.

**Definition 48 (envy).** *Envy* is hatred in so far as it induces a person to be pained by another good fortune (E3 Definitions of the Emotions, 23).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.

- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E3 Definitions of the Emotions, 23.
