**Compassion** *(definition, id: D-compassion)*.

**Why this follows.** Social affect.

**Definition 49 (compassion).** *Compassion* (pity) is sorrow accompanied by the idea of evil that has happened to another whom we imagine like ourselves (E3 Definitions of the Emotions, 18 pattern).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
