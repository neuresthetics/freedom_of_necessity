**What substance means** *(definition, id: D-substance)*.

**Why this follows.** After Nature taken as one (D-Nature), Spinoza Part I ground needs the word substance fixed without scoring the full metaphysics yet.

**Definition 20 (substance).** A *substance* is that which is in itself and is conceived through itself: that is, that the conception of which does not need the conception of another thing from which it must be formed (E1D3).

**Working sense in this chain.** *Nature taken as one* (D-Nature) is the only candidate that meets this definition without remainder. Finite organizations graded by *Whole...* (D-whole) are not substances in this sense.

**Declared reading (unscored).** That Nature is the one substance (E1P14) remains declared inside D-Nature. This definition names the term; it does not score uniqueness.

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
