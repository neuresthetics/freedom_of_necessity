**The state exists to secure common power of acting** *(proposition, id: P14)*.

**Why this follows.** *The state* (D-state) and *The political order is a whole to a degree* (A8) need an end in this chain's work-language, tied to virtue as power (P12).

**Proposition 14.** The state (*The state*, D-state; A8) exists, in this chain's working sense, to secure common power of acting — peace and security as *good* (*Good*, D-good) for its members — not to cancel natural striving (*Striving to persist*, D-strive) but to organize it (*Natural right as power*, D-right).

**Stated sense.** End-of-the-state claim for this chain. Does not score the best constitution.

**How P14 fails.** If A8 fails, or if the state's end is shown to be unrelated to common power of acting in this sense.

**Cap.** Cites A4, A8, and P12. Effective confidence = min(A4, A8, P12) once scored. Definitions add nothing.
