**Adequate idea** *(definition, id: D-adequate-idea)*.

**Why this follows.** Reason and intuition need adequate ideas named.

**Definition 36 (adequate idea).** An *adequate idea* is an idea which, insofar as it is considered in itself without relation to its object, has all the properties or intrinsic marks of a true idea (E2D4).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.

- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E2D4.
