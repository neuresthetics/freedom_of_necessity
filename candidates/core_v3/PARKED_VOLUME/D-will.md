**Will as affirmation** *(definition, id: D-will)*.

**Why this follows.** A5 denies uncaused choice; Part II needs will named as affirmation.

**Definition 30 (will).** *Will* is the mind affirming or denying; volitions are not a faculty separate from ideas (E2P48-P49 pattern).

**Link to A5.** An uncaused will would lower A5. This definition refuses that faculty picture.

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.

**References.**
- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E2P48; E2P49.
