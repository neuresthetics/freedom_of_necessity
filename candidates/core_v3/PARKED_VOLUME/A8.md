**The political order is a whole to a degree** *(axiom, model, id: A8)*.

**Why this follows.** *The society I live in is a whole to a degree* (A4) grades society. Politics needs the same claim under state form (*The state*, D-state).

**Axiom.** The political order (*The state*, D-state) I live under performs each of the seven works (*Work, and the seven works*, D-work) as one, at least in part, and I am one of its members — the sense of *The society I live in is a whole to a degree* (A4) under political form.

**Stated sense.** Model axiom: A4 applied to the commonwealth I live under. Does not score which constitution is best. Membership uses the same sense as A4 (I share the whole's fate and take part in its works).

**What would lower it.** Evidence that the political order I live under fails every work graded as one, or that I am not a member in A4's membership sense.

**Cap.** Cites A4. Effective confidence = min(own score, A4) once scored. Definitions add nothing.
