**No separate uncaused faculty of will** *(proposition, id: P9)*.

**Why this follows.** *Every event is caused* (A5) rules out uncaused will. *Will as affirmation* (D-will) names willing as affirming or denying ideas. This proposition states the punchline on this chain (E2P48 pattern).

**Proposition 9.** There is no separate uncaused faculty of will. Willing is the affirming or denying of ideas (*Will as affirmation*, D-will) under causes (*Every event is caused*, A5).

**Stated sense.** Denies a libertarian faculty above ideas. Does not deny that people affirm and deny; it denies that affirmation is uncaused.

**How P9 fails.** If A5 fails at the scale of choices, or if will is shown to be a separate uncaused faculty, Proposition 9 falls.

**Cap.** Cites A5. Effective confidence = A5 once scored. D-will is a definition and adds nothing.
