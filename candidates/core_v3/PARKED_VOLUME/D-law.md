**Law as common decree** *(definition, id: D-law)*.

**Why this follows.** State needs law.

**Definition 60 (law).** *Civil law* is a common decree of the commonwealth by which living is prescribed to the members of the state (TTP/TP pattern).

**Natural vs civil.** Natural right (D-right) is power; civil law channels power into common rules. Neither word here settles theology.

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
