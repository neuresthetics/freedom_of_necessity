**Active affects follow from own organization** *(proposition, id: P10)*.

**Why this follows.** *Affect, and change of power* (D-affect) and *Adequate and partial cause* (D-adequate-cause) give the active/passive pattern. This proposition states it for the A2 agent under P3.

**Proposition 10.** An affect in the agent of *A human body, and its mind* (A2) is *active* to the degree that the change in power of acting (*Affect, and change of power*, D-affect) follows from the agent's own organization as adequate cause (*Adequate and partial cause*, D-adequate-cause), under *Freedom by degrees under necessity* (P3).

**Stated sense.** Degree claim parallel to D-free Definition 15(a). Passive is the residual when the agent is only a partial cause.

**How P10 fails.** If P3 fails, or if active/passive cannot be graded by adequate cause in this chain's terms.

**Cap.** Cites A2 and P3. Effective confidence = min(A2, P3) once scored. Definitions add nothing.
