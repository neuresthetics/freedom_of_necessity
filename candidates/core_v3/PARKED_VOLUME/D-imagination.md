**Imagination** *(definition, id: D-imagination)*.

**Why this follows.** Three kinds of knowledge need the first rung named.

**Definition 31 (imagination).** *Imagination* is the mind regarding of external bodies through the ideas of affections of the human body, as if those bodies were present (E2P17 pattern; E2P40 Scholium 2 first kind).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.

**References.**
- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E2P17; E2P40 Scholium 2.
