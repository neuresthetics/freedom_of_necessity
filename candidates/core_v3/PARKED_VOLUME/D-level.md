**Levels of organization** *(definition, id: D-level)*.

**Why this follows.** *Whole...* (D-whole) already uses "level"; this entry expands room for many layers so P1 and later entries share one sense.

**Definition 64 (level).** A *level* is a grain of description at which something can be graded as a whole (*Whole, degree, member, level, self-production, sameness*, D-whole) and can stand as a member of a whole at a coarser grain. Examples in this chain: cell to multicellular body to household/workplace to society/state. The stack of levels stops short of *Nature taken as one* (D-Nature): in Nature is not member of Nature.

**Order.** Definition expands D-whole; *A whole at one level, a part at the next* (P1) uses it. Mentions of P1 in scholia are forward pointers, not cites.

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
