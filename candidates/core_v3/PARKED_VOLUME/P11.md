**Passive affects bind when causes stay unclear** *(proposition, id: P11)*.

**Why this follows.** *Bondage is acting from what is outside* (P4) is the inverse of degree-freedom. Passive affects are the affect-side of that inverse when causes stay inadequate.

**Proposition 11.** Passive affects bind the agent — that is, raise *Bondage is acting from what is outside* (P4) — to the degree their causes are known only inadequately (*Inadequate idea*, D-inadequate-idea) rather than through the agent's own organization (P3).

**Stated sense.** Bridge from affect taxonomy to P4. Does not claim every sorrow is bondage, or that naming an affect frees the agent.

**How P11 fails.** If P4's inverse claim fails, or if inadequate ideas never track bondage in this chain's sense.

**Cap.** Cites A2, P3, and P4. Effective confidence = min(A2, P3, P4) once scored. Definitions add nothing.
