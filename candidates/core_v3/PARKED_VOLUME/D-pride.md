**Pride** *(definition, id: D-pride)*.

**Why this follows.** Self-regarding affect.

**Definition 46 (pride).** *Pride* is joy born of the fact that a person thinks too highly of themselves (E3 Definitions of the Emotions, 28).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
