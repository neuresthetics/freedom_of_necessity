**Striving to persist** *(definition, id: D-strive)*.

**Why this follows freedom.** *Freedom by degrees under necessity* (P3) says a finite agent is freer when what it does follows from its own organization and from knowledge of causes. Spinoza's next ground is that each thing strives to persist in its being (E3P6–P7). This definition fixes that claim in the book's own terms — the seven works — so later entries on affect, bondage, and blessedness can use it without importing a second ontology.

**Definition 16 (strive).** A finite organization *strives to persist in its being* when it continues the works that keep it going (*Work, and the seven works*, D-work), as far as those works go. The striving *is* those works under way, not a further force beside them.

**Explanation.**

- *In its being.* For a finite whole (*Whole, degree, member, level, self-production, sameness*, D-whole), "being" here means lasting as that organization: going on existing while its degree is above zero (*What exists*, D-exist; D-whole).
- *Not a second engine.* The book does not posit a conatus over and above energy intake, maintenance, control, signaling, memory, reproduction, and defense. Where those works are performed as one, the organization is striving; where they fail, the striving fails with them.
- *Body and society.* The definition applies to any finite organization D-work grades — including the body *A human body, and its mind* (A2) scores, and the society *The society I live in is a whole to a degree* (A4) scores — each at its own level. It does not say the society feels its striving.

**Report (Spinoza, not scored).** "Everything, in so far as it is in itself, endeavours to persist in its own being" (E3P6). "The endeavour, wherewith everything endeavours to persist in its own being, is nothing else but the actual essence of the thing in question" (E3P7). Definition 16 takes that pair as the pattern and restates it in the book's work-language. It does not derive E3P6–P7, and it adds no confidence.

**What D-strive leaves out.**

- *Why anything is felt.* A2's limit stays open; striving as works does not explain feeling.
- *Will as uncaused.* Striving is compatible with *Every event is caused* (A5); it is not a libertarian will.
- *Strict freedom.* Only Nature is free in the strict sense (*Freedom, strict and by degree*, D-free, Definition 14). Striving does not make a finite thing self-caused (E1D1 vs D-whole Definition 11; D-Nature, Scholium 3).

**Limit.** This is a definition. It fixes what "strive" means in the book. It does not make P3 or any claim more likely.
