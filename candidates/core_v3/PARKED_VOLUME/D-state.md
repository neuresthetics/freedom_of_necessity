**Why this follows.** A4 named society; politics needs state.

**Definition 59 (state).** The *state* (commonwealth) is a society (*The society I live in is a whole to a degree*, A4) organized by common laws so that the collective power of acting secures peace and the conditions of striving for its members (TP / TTP pattern).
