**Bad** *(definition, id: D-bad)*.

**Why this follows.** Pair with good.

**Definition 54 (bad).** By *bad* (evil) I mean that which we certainly know to be a hindrance to our possession of what is good (E4D2) — in this chain, what diminishes power of acting (D-affect).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
