**Activity and passivity** *(definition, id: D-activity)*.

**Why this follows.** Everyday words for E3D2.

**Definition 67 (activity / passivity).** *Activity* is being the adequate cause of what happens in or through an organization; *passivity* is being only a partial cause (*Adequate and partial cause*, D-adequate-cause). Affects are active or passive in the same sense (*Affect...*, D-affect).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
