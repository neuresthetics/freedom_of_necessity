**Idea** *(definition, id: D-idea)*.

**Why this follows.** Mind-as-organization (A2) needs the Part II word for mental acts.

**Definition 29 (idea).** An *idea* is a conception of the mind which the mind forms because it is a thinking thing (E2D3).

**Note.** Conception over perception marks that idea is an act of the mind. On A2 declared reading, that act is the body organization described in the first person — not a second substance product.

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.

- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E2D3.
