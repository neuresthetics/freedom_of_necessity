**Essence** *(definition, id: D-essence)*.

**Why this follows.** Substance/mode talk needs essence before good, virtue, and eternity.

**Definition 24 (essence).** The *essence* of a thing is that which, being given, the thing is necessarily posited, and which, being taken away, the thing is necessarily taken away; or that without which the thing can neither be nor be conceived, and vice versa (E2D2).

**Link to striving.** *Striving to persist* (D-strive) continues the works that keep an organization going; in Spinoza pattern, striving is the essence insofar as conceived to follow from the given essence (E3P7). Report pattern, not a new scored law.

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.

**References.**
- Spinoza, B. *Ethics*: E2D2; E3P7.
