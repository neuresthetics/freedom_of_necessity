**Virtue as power** *(definition, id: D-virtue)*.

**Why this follows.** Part IV keyword before free-person props.

**Definition 57 (virtue).** By *virtue* and *power* I mean the same thing: virtue, insofar as it relates to us, is our essence or nature insofar as we have the power of effecting things that can be understood through our nature alone (E4D8 pattern). In this chain: power of acting (D-affect) under degree-freedom (D-free Definition 15).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
