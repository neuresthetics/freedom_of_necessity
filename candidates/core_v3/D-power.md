**Power of acting** *(definition, id: D-power)*.

**Why this follows.** A single home for the phrase used everywhere.

**Definition 68 (power of acting).** *Power of acting* is the degree to which an organization performs the works that keep it going (*Work...*, D-work; *Whole...*, D-whole) while it strives (*Striving to persist*, D-strive). Synonym family: perfection in the finite sense, virtue as power.

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
