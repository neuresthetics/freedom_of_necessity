**Why this follows.** Fill E2P40 Schol. 2 first-kind sources.

**Definition 65 (experience).** *Undirected experience* is acquaintance with singular things without adequate ordering of causes; it feeds knowledge of the first kind (*Three kinds of knowledge*, D-knowledge; E2P40 Scholium 2).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
