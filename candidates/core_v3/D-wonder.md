**Wonder** *(definition, id: D-wonder)*.

**Why this follows.** Attention affect.

**Definition 51 (wonder).** *Wonder* is the imagination of a thing wherein the mind remains fixed because this singular imagination has no connection with the others (E3 Definitions of the Emotions, 4).

**Limit.** Definition. Adds no confidence.
