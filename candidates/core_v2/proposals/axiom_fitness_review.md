## A1. "I exist."

**(1) Job and drop test.** A1 gives the chains their subject and marks the one point that cannot be revised. A2 cites it. *Drop A1:* no demonstration step fails. A2's own evidence is first-person ("closing these eyes ends my seeing"), and gathering it is already a case of thinking going on, so A2 entails what A1 says. P1 and P2 reach A1 only through A2. What would be lost is structural, not deductive: the book's one fixed point (verse 149), the place where the chain splits for an AI (A1, Scholium 1), and the guarantee that "I" names something.

**(2) Weakest version?** Yes. It says only that this thinking is going on, and so its thinker, if there is one distinct from it, exists now. It claims nothing about kind, duration or other minds.

**(3) Independent?** Yes. One small point: the sentence "An activity cannot go on while what does it, if anything does, fails to exist; this is taken as evident with the root" is a second premise. It is needed only on D-I's first clause (a thinker distinct from the thinking). On the second clause it is not needed at all.

**(4) Outside evidence.** None can reach it, by design. That is acceptable only because A1 caps nothing and lends nothing: its immunity buys no confidence for anything downstream. Lichtenberg's objection (K 76) is the standing challenge, and A1 already concedes it through D-I's second clause.

**(5) Proposal (optional tidy, not needed).** Replace the bridging sentence with:

> On D-I's second clause nothing more is needed: the thinking that is going on is what exists. On the first clause one step is added and taken as evident with the root: an activity cannot go on while what does it fails to exist.

**Verdict: keep.**

## A2. "I am a human body made of cells, and my mind is that same body's organization described in the first person."

**(1) Job and drop test.** A2 supplies the body that P1 calls a whole, and it ties A1's I to that body so that the whole of P1, step 3, and the member of A4 are one thing.

- *Drop A2:* P1 fails at step 1 (no body), step 4 (no cells of it) and step 6 (no reason the member is the whole). A4's membership clause loses its subject. P2 keeps its rows but loses the A2 link that fixes "the society I live in".
- *Drop only the declared reading (identity, and mind as organization):* nothing fails. P1, Scholium 4, already says "Step 6 needs only that each thought's thinking goes with this one body, not that I am identical with it." The declared reading is used only in P1, step 8's last sentence, and in scholia (P2, Scholia 1 and 4).

**(2) Weakest version?** No. The statement claims more than is scored or used. "I am a human body" reads as identity, and "my mind is that same body's organization" is the Spinozist reading (after E2P7 Scholium, E3P2 Scholium). Both are unscored by A2's own text. The statement is the first thing a reader sees, so it gives the strong reading the look of an axiom. Also, P1 needs one thing A2 does not quite say: that *whenever* I think, the thinking goes with this same body. A2 gives the evidence for it (and P1, step 6, uses it) but the statement speaks only of the present.

"Human" is more than P1 needs ("multicellular organism" would do), but it is cheap, well evidenced and central to the book, so keep it.

**(3) Independent?** Yes. It cites A1 and borrows "organization" from D-work, Definition 3, by a forward pointer, which is a gloss, not a dependency of support.

**(4) Outside evidence.** Clause (a) can be reached. It would be lowered by specific mental abilities that do not vary with the organization of the nervous system: for example, a well-documented case of an ability surviving the destruction of the structure it otherwise depends on, or this thinking going with no single body. The declared reading cannot be reached by any observation, and A2 says so: the evidence fits "produces" and "is" equally. That is honest, but it is a reason not to put it in the axiom's statement.

**(5) Proposal: split.** Scored axiom:

> **A2.** Whenever I think, the thinking goes on with one human body, made of cells and of what cells make. I am made of that body, and the thinking varies with its organization.

Declared reading, kept in A2 as a labelled section or moved to its own entry once HOW_THIS_BOOK_IS_BUILT.md has a kind for declared readings (see README, open question 12):

> **Declared reading (unscored).** I am that body, and my mind is that same body's organization described in the first person: one thing described two ways (E2P7 Scholium; E2P13; E3P2 Scholium), not a second thing it produces.

This reopens wording Jason accepted earlier (README, open question 1). It is proposed only because he asked for everything to be on the table. The book's thesis is unchanged; only the label on its strongest sentence changes.

**Verdict: split.**

## A3. "The seven works sort things the way self-production does."

**(1) Job and drop test.** A3 does two jobs.

- *Job A:* it gives P1, step 2, the premise that this body performs all seven works as one. P1 reaches it by a detour: a multicellular organism produces itself ("read as in A3's caution on the anchors"), and so, by A3, performs all seven.
- *Job B:* it checks the seven-work measure against an independent test, self-production, so that "whole" in D-whole tracks something and is not a list that sorts by fiat.

*Drop A3:* P1 fails at step 2, so both halves of P1 fall (the society's level is defined relative to a whole body, D-whole, Definition 10). P2 is untouched. *But* step 2 could be supplied directly: A3's own anchor bullets already give, row by row, the body-level evidence (skin and mucosa; nervous and endocrine systems; digestion and respiration; blood and lymph; nerves and hormones; the immune system; synaptic memory). With that, P1 stands without the self-production thesis, without the negative controls, and without the contested reading that multicellular organisms are self-producing in the first-order sense, which Maturana and Varela left open (A3's own caution).

*Drop Job B only:* no demonstration fails. What is lost is the claim that the measure means something. A4's "whole" would then mean only "performs the seven works as one", by stipulation.

**(2) Weakest version?** Not for P1. By METHOD rule 1, P1 is capped by A3's weakest test result, which includes the self-recharging robot, the mature red blood cell and bacterial transport. None of these bear on whether a human body performs the seven. P1 inherits risk it does not need.

For Job B, the strong form is the right one: it is the riskier and more informative claim, and PR1 predicts where it will fail first (below).

**(3) Independent?** Partly. D-work, Definition 3(b) ("carried out by structures that the thing's organization itself produces or maintains") is self-production asked one work at a time. A3 admits this ("to that extent less of a discovery"). The cases split:

- The stone, the hurricane, the archive and the candle flame fail on the *content* of the works (no energy intake, no sorting edge, no memory). That is independent evidence for Job B.
- The self-recharging robot passes every activity question and fails *only* by condition (b). Its agreement with self-production is built in, so it is not independent evidence.

The two tests can also come apart in the other direction, and this is new. Because (b) says "produces *or* maintains" (kept so that a transplanted organ counts), a machine that keeps itself going by swapping in parts made elsewhere can pass (b) without making anything. A real first step exists. UBTech's Walker S2 humanoid (2025) removes its own spent battery and fits a charged one from a swap station, with no person involved (heise online, 21 July 2025; New Atlas, 21 July 2025). On energy, its organization arguably maintains the structure that does the work. It still fails the other works, since people make and repair its casing, controller and sensors, so the predicted degree stays 0. But a robot that replaced every worn module from factory stock would pass (b) on all seven by "maintains" while making none of its components: an F2 case. There is a tradeoff here, not an error. The closer (b) is to Definition 11, the safer A3 and the less it finds out. The looser it is, the more A3 risks, and the more its agreement means.

A3 also claims more than PR1 explains. PR1 holds transport, signaling and defense to be required only in some circumstances. A3 says every self-producing thing performs all seven. So PR1 predicts A3's first F1 failures: a small cell that moves materials only by diffusion (A3 already lists this), or a self-producing thing grown where no damaging agents are present. Whether such a thing "performs" defense depends on whether standing detection counts as the activity going on (D-work, Definition 4). That should be stated.

**(4) Outside evidence.** Yes, and in three named ways.

- *F1:* a self-producing thing graded 0 on some work, likely transport or defense.
- *F2:* a thing that does not produce itself graded above 0 on all seven. A self-maintaining robot is the live candidate.
- *F3:* graders working apart from the same evidence disagree about zeros.

An actual inter-grader study would be the cheapest evidence that could lower it.

**(5) Proposal: split.**

> **A3a (axiom, empirical).** The human body performs each of the seven works as one (D-work, Definitions 5 and 6).
>
> Evidence: the body halves of A3's present anchor bullets, unchanged. Cited by P1. No self-production premise.

> **A3b (model test of D-work and D-whole).** Where self-production (D-whole, Definition 11) can be checked, the seven works sort things as it does: a thing that produces itself performs all seven as one; a thing that lasts without producing itself fails at least one.
>
> Anchors, controls, hard cases and F1–F3 as now. Cited by no demonstration. If it fails, D-work or D-whole is revised and every entry that uses them is rescored, as A3 already says.

Add to A3b's hard cases:

> *A robot that swaps its own parts.* UBTech's Walker S2 (2025) replaces its own battery at a swap station. Under Definition 3(b)'s "or maintains", its energy work may count as its own; people still make and repair its casing, controller and sensors, so its predicted degree is 0. A robot that replaced every worn module from factory stock would pass (b) on all seven while making none of its components. That would be F2, unless "maintains" is read to exclude fitting a part made elsewhere.

Add to A3b's Limits:

> The controls that fail on what the works are (stone, hurricane, archive, flame) are independent evidence. The robot fails only by condition (b), which is self-production asked one work at a time, so its result is not.

Also, one sentence for D-work, Definition 4, to settle the defense question:

> A work of detection is going on while the thing's detectors are active, whether or not anything is detected.

**Verdict: split.**

## A4. "The society I live in is a whole to a degree, judged one work at a time: it performs each of the seven works as one, at least in part, and I am one of its members."

**(1) Job and drop test.** A4 does three jobs: the society performs all seven works as one, at least in part (for P1); it performs control, signaling and memory as one (for P2); and I am a member (for P1).

- *Drop A4:* P1's second half fails (steps 5 and 7); its first half, "I am a whole", stands. P2 fails entirely.
- *Drop the four rows P2 does not use (boundary, energy, transport, defense):* P2 stands. P1 fails, because the society is no longer shown to be a whole (D-whole, Definition 8), and so it cannot have members (Definition 9) or be a level (Definition 10).
- *Drop membership:* P2 stands. P1 fails at steps 5 and 6.

**(2) Weakest version?** Partly.

- Grade 1 or more on each row is already the weakest claim that makes the society a whole, and P1 needs all seven for that. The real cost is that P1's second half is hostage to the boundary and defense rows, which A4's own scholium says "lean mainly on the state" and "may look weak". P2 does not need them.
- Membership is bundled in. It is a claim about *me*, with different evidence (my food, my public health, my work), and it is the only reason A4 cites A2. Bundled, it makes P2 formally capped by A2 for no reason P2 uses.

**(3) Independent?** Two problems.

- *The take-apart test leans on "institution" without a scale.* The declared reading counts "all its institutions, state and non-state", including families and firms. But Definition 5 counts a work as the society's only when it is not carried "only by each of its components for its own sake", and a family or firm is a component. As written, households feeding themselves could be counted as the society's energy work. The Somalia case (Leeson 2007) shows the danger. When the state went, trade and telecommunications went on through clans, firms and markets. Whether those count for A4 should turn on whether they worked at the society's scale, not on whether they were institutions. Without that, almost any collapse leaves A4 standing, and A4 becomes hard to vary only inside the system.
- *A4 leans on PR1 beyond PR1's scope.* "Why the same seven works should turn up here at all is PR1's explanation." PR1 is stated about self-producing things (D-whole, Definition 11). The core does not claim the society produces itself; A3 says self-production is "hard to check directly for large, loose things like a society". So the sentence assumes something nowhere claimed.

**(4) Outside evidence.** Yes, through historical take-apart cases rather than experiments. A row would be lowered by a well-documented breakdown of the institutions carrying a work at the society's scale, after which the activity went on unchanged in each household or person: the members doing it separately. State collapses, long blackouts, supply-chain failures and epidemics with collapsed public health are the obvious places to look. F3 (graders disagreeing about a society row) also applies. That can only be tested once the scale criterion below is in place.

**(5) Proposal: split and adjust.**

> **A4 (axiom, model).** The society I live in is a whole to a degree, judged one work at a time: it performs each of the seven works as one, at least in part.

> **A5 (axiom, empirical; cites A2).** I am a member of that society: the lasting of the body I am made of depends on several of its works, and I take part in some of them (D-whole, Definition 9).
>
> Evidence: A4's present Membership paragraph, unchanged.

Add to A4's declared reading:

> An institution counts toward a row only where it works at the society's scale: it connects or serves members across the society, not only one household, firm or clan for itself. "The society I live in" only picks out which society is meant; it carries no evidence.

Replace "Why the same seven works should turn up here at all is PR1's explanation, which adds nothing to any row." with:

> PR1 would explain why the seven turn up here only if the society produced itself, which the core does not claim. Here PR1 only says where to look.

This is a third answer to README, open question 13 (keep membership in A4, or move it to P1): give it its own entry.

**Verdict: split and adjust.**

## PR1. "Where the same requirement holds, the same work turns up, in forms that differ completely from level to level."

**(1) Job and drop test.** PR1 explains why A3's anchors all perform the seven, says where to look at a new level, and carries the book's distinction between requirement and modal necessity. *Drop PR1:* nothing fails. By METHOD rule 4 it adds no confidence, and no demonstration cites it. What is lost is the explanation and the book's account of requirement, which matters to the title.

**(2) Weakest version?** Yes, after the fourth round's sorting into general requirements, conditional ones and the weak memory claim. Two parts are nearly true by definition, and the text should say so of both, not only of the edge:

- the edge (already marked);
- control: "To keep its many works in step with one another, it must coordinate them" is close to analytic, since keeping works in step is coordinating them.

**(3) Independent?** Yes. It is stated about self-producing things (Definition 11), not about wholes, which avoids the old emptiness. Its only reach beyond its scope is in A4 (above), and the fix belongs there.

**(4) Outside evidence.** Yes, and the entry names it: a self-producing thing that lasts while lacking a general work, or one that lacks a conditional work where the condition holds (a large one with no transport, one among damaging agents with no defense). Any such case is also an F1 failure of A3b.

**(5) Proposal (optional).** In the first bullet under *General*, add after "it must coordinate them (control)":

> This one is close to true by definition, since keeping works in step is what coordinating them means; the content is that a self-producing thing has many works.

**Verdict: keep.**

## P2, as it bears on the axioms

P2's demonstration uses only A4's control, signaling and memory rows, and its Cap paragraph says it is capped by those three. But METHOD rule 1 says an entry is capped by everything it cites, and a multi-row entry by its weakest row. So P2, citing A4, is formally capped by A4's weakest row, possibly boundary or defense, which P2 never uses. The entry and the rule disagree. Two ways to fix it:

- **Row-level citation (preferred).** `cites: [A4 (control, signaling, memory)]`, with a matching sentence added to METHOD.md, rule 1: "An entry that cites named rows of a multi-row entry is capped by those rows only." METHOD.md is not edited here; this is flagged for the author.
- **Recast as a corollary.** Move the meaning of "collective mind" into a definition, and state P2 as a corollary of A4: "To the degree that A4's control, signaling and memory rows hold, a collective mind in the sense defined happens." This makes plain that P2 adds a name, not a finding.

With A5 split out, P2 no longer needs the cap through A2. "The society I live in" is fixed by A4's declared reading and carries no evidence.

**Verdict: adjust (row-level citation), with merge-as-corollary as the alternative.**

## Definitions the axioms lean on

- **D-work, Definition 3(b), "produces or maintains."** Kept to count a transplanted organ. It also counts a machine that fits parts made elsewhere, which is the new F2 risk above. If the author wants the robot verdict to be safe rather than tested, change "maintains" to "maintains by its own activity, from materials it takes in", which still counts a transplanted organ (the body feeds and repairs it) and excludes swapping in a factory part. Recommended: keep the present wording and add the hard case, so the risk is visible.
- **D-work, Definition 4, "performs."** Defines performing as the activity going on. Defense and memory are partly standing capacities. The sentence proposed under A3 settles it.
- **D-work, Definition 5, the take-apart test.** Applied to a society, it needs the scale criterion proposed under A4, or households and firms count as the society's own works.
- **D-whole, Definition 8, the lowest grade.** This is why P1's second half needs all seven of the society's rows. It is the right rule (a hurricane's energy does not give it a memory). Its cost should be stated where P1 is: the boundary and defense rows decide whether I am a part at the next level.
- **D-whole, Definition 11, "make and replace."** It is the independent test only as far as it differs from condition (b). The difference ("make", not only "maintain") is what the robot case turns on.
