**Principle.** Where the same requirement holds, the same work turns up, in forms that differ completely from level to level.

**Two words.** *Requirement* here is conditional: *if* a thing that produces itself is to go on producing itself, *then* it must do this work. *Necessity*, the word in the book's title, is modal: what could not be otherwise. PR1 uses only the first. In the core, "must" appears only in this entry.

**The thesis.** Each of the seven works answers a requirement of anything that produces itself (D-whole, Definition 11). The requirements are not all of one strength, and they are sorted here so that none claims more than it can bear.

*General: requirements of any self-producing thing.*

- To make up for running down, it must take in energy (energy). Making and replacing components takes work, and nothing that runs down goes on doing work without intake.
- To keep its many works in step with one another, it must coordinate them (control).
- To stay distinct from what surrounds it while materials cross, its edge must sort, letting some things across and turning others back (boundary). That it has an edge at all is nearly true by definition, since Definition 11 counts the components of its edge among the components a self-producing thing makes. The requirement is in the sorting.

*Holding in some circumstances: requirements only where a condition is met.*

- Where components lie far from the edge where materials come in, farther than diffusion can serve, it must move materials (transport). In very small cells diffusion may be enough (A3, hard cases).
- Where one component's state must reach a component at a distance, it must carry information (signaling).
- Where agents that damage it are present, it must resist them (defense). Every known living thing meets such agents, but the requirement is conditional on them.

*Memory, two claims.* "Keep a record of how its components are made" can mean two things.

- *Stored information about how its components are made, used again in rebuilding them.* This is D-work's memory work applied to rebuilding. Anything that rebuilds its components at all has this, and the information need not sit in a separate record, so the requirement is nearly empty, though not quite: it rules out a thing that would have to be rebuilt from nothing. PR1 keeps this as the requirement (memory).
- *A separate stored record*, like a genome, apart from the components it describes. That is not required. Models of mutually catalytic assemblies pass on their composition with no sequence-based record at all (Segré, Ben-Eli & Lancet 2000). Those are models, not living things. They show only that the stronger claim is not true by definition. That every known living thing keeps such a record is a finding about them.

A membrane and a border look nothing alike. PR1 says why both turn up: the same requirement holds at both levels, and what carries across is the work, not the form.

**What PR1 is for.** It explains why A3's anchors all perform the seven, and it tells a reader where to look at a new level: for each requirement, find what does that work there. The details are found at that level, not assumed.

**What would count against it.** For a general requirement: a self-producing thing that lasts while lacking that work, or one that loses it and goes on producing itself. For a requirement that holds in some circumstances: such a thing in those circumstances (a large one with no transport, one among damaging agents with no defense). Any such case would also be an F1 failure of A3. A self-producing thing that lacks a conditional work where the condition is absent counts against neither.

**Limit.** An explanation adds no confidence (METHOD.md, rule 4). A3's anchors passing shows the works are *performed*; it does not show they are *required*, and PR1 cannot lend A3 or A4 any score. Each row earns its grade from its own evidence.

**What changed.** The earlier PR1 spoke of "any whole that persists". Now that D-whole defines a whole by the seven works, "a whole must perform the seven works" would be true by definition and empty. So the thesis is stated about self-producing things, which D-whole, Definition 11, defines without the seven works.
