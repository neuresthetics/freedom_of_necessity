I am a body made of cells, organized by type and function (neurons, immune cells, muscle, and so on), and that organization produces my mind.

Evidence: cell theory (Schleiden and Schwann 1838-39, Virchow 1855) holds that all living bodies are made of cells. The human body has roughly 30 trillion of them (Sender, Fuchs, and Milo 2016), differentiated into specialized types. Neuroscience ties mental function to the organization of neurons: damage to specific brain regions removes specific mental abilities (for example Broca 1861, patient H.M. 1957), which shows that the organization is enough to give rise to them, and changing neural activity changes experience. Caveat: that organization "produces" mind is the working position of neuroscience, not a solved question; the hard problem of consciousness remains open.
