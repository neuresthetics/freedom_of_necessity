**Claim.** I function within that society as a cell. A cell acts on the body not alone but through tissues and organs; in the same way, I act on society through groups and organizations (families, firms, institutions). So at one level I am a cell-like unit, and the organ-like working parts of society are groups and organizations, not individuals.
