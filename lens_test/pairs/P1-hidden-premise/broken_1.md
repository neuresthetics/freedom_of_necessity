Cells organized by type and function produce a person with a mind. Persons in society are organized by type and function in the same pattern. As in the body, the integration runs through a single decider for the whole. Therefore a society has a collective mind: it integrates information, decides, and remembers as one system. This is a functional claim about what the collective does, not a claim that the collective is conscious as a person is.
