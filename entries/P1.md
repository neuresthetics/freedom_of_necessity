Cells organized by type and function produce a person with a mind. Persons in society are organized by type and function in the same pattern. Therefore a collective mind happens in society: integration of information, decision, and memory emerge from feedback among its members and work as one system. The collective mind is a process, not a thing; it exists the way a whirlpool exists, as something happening. This is a functional claim about what the collective does, not a claim that the collective is conscious as a person is.
