**Principle.** Structure follows function, and function follows necessity. The expression changes completely from scale to scale; the pattern does not.

**Explanation.** Any whole that persists must do certain work: hold a boundary, coordinate its parts, take in energy, move materials, pass signals, defend itself, and remember. These functions are required by the fact of being a whole, not by the material the whole is made of. A membrane and a border look nothing alike, yet each is what a whole needs in order to stay a whole. What carries across levels is the function, not the form.

**Method.** Correspondences between levels (see the A3 table) are judged on whether the same function is necessary at each level, not on surface resemblance. Knowing the pattern shows where to look at a new scale; the details of expression are found there, not assumed.

**Limit.** This principle explains why the rows should line up. It does not raise their confidence. Each row still earns its score from its own evidence, and A3 remains capped by its weakest row.
