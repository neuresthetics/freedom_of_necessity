Society exhibits organism-level properties across seven rows. Evidence backbone: Miller, Living Systems (1978), which defines the same critical subsystems (boundary, decider, converter, distributor, channel and net, memory) at every level from cell to society. Caveats: the person is not a direct component of society (groups and organizations sit between), and cells are near-clonal and cooperative while persons keep their own interests and conflict (Queller and Strassmann 2009).
