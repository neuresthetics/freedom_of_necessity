# Foreword (draft) — How this text came to be

*Working draft for a final-version foreword. Not yet promoted into the published front matter. Companion to [PREFACE.md](../PREFACE.md) (lost original / method borrowed); this piece is the origin story of **this** book.*

---

There is a short answer to how this text came to be, and a longer one. The short answer is: it started from one line that cannot be taken back — *I exist* — and grew by checking every next step against what came before.

The longer answer is below.

## Origin

Jason Burns set the starting point, the method, and the direction. He did not write most of the pages by hand. The bulk of the drafting and checking was done with **digital intelligence** (Grok), under rules he kept: what may be claimed, what must rest on what, what counts as a pass, and what gets parked when it fails.

He accepted steps, rejected steps, and sent the work back when a claim overreached. A few lines are his in wording as well as in intent — among them the old companion verses on freedom (143 and 144), kept at **V013 · P3**, and the “lamp, not a proof” line (86), whose final home in the live set is still open. Those stay credited as author-written. Everything else in the chain is collaborative under his veto.

The thesis is not that Spinoza was wrong about free will. It is that he was **incomplete** on it. Spinoza denied a will that starts things from nowhere (E1P32; E2P48) and placed freedom in acting from the necessity of one’s own nature (E1D7). This book keeps that frame and works it out further than his day could — with evidence about bodies, minds, and societies that he did not have, and with digital intelligence as a drafting and checking partner he could not have imagined.

## Method

The shape is geometric, in the manner of the *Ethics*: definitions, axioms, propositions; each entry cites what it rests on; confidence cannot rise above the weakest support down the chain. Spinoza is cited by part and proposition only.

What this book adds is the **collision record**. For a live claim, rival sides are argued at full strength; each side says in advance what would prove it wrong; only what survives on checked evidence stays. What fails is parked or rewritten in the open. In that sense the collision record *is* the book — not a sidecar to a finished manuscript, but the manuscript as it is being tested.

The core rebuild now in play (`candidates/core_v3/`, snapshot `core-v3-snapshot-v1`) still starts at **A1**: *I exist*. Only that root is scored absolute. The rest waits on the collider. Older promoted seed material remains in `entries/` until a separate promotion is accepted. Logs, results folders, and tags keep the trail — this is a living document of entries, collisions, versions, and verse faces, not a sealed volume.

## Dual render

Readers meet the same chain through two doors.

- The **technical** face shows the joints: dependencies, caps, cites, entry ids.  
- The **poetic** face carries the same claims in plain modern lines — short, Dhammapada-like, with analogy only when it helps.

Same `V001`–`V251`, same index labels. Not two books; one chain, two ways in. Spinoza’s own geometric form is exact, and the living sense of the *Ethics* is not always visible under the proofs — he says as much himself. The dual render is how this book keeps both.

When a new rendition is chosen, the work does **not** score the two tracks against each other. Variation and hardness checks run on the **combined** technical-and-structure chain. One winner is picked. **Then** that winner gets the dual render treatment — technical and poetic together, same numbers and labels.

## What comes next

The next turn of the screw is **hardness-to-vary**: controlled variants of the combined chain, measured for what breaks under paraphrase, reorder, synonym swap, and dependency remap. The point is to see which parts of the structure are load-bearing and which are surface — then keep a single winner and give it the dual render again.

No harness scores yet beyond A1. No claim that the book is finished. The method stays open: anyone can read the entries, the collisions, the logs, and the verse faces, and push on a weak joint.

If the original *Ethics* is lost, the method was never lost with it. This text is one more walk of that order, started from *I exist*, checked in public, and still under way.

— *Draft foreword, 2026-10-25*
