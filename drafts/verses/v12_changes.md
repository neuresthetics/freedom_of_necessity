## What's new

- **Order.** v11 opened with God, or Nature, and reached the self at verse 56. v12 opens where core v2 does: "I exist" (1), then what the I is (A2), then works, wholes, Nature, the test, the pattern, society, levels, the collective mind, then the first person, freedom, God or Nature, and the close.
- **The root.** A1 is "I exist." v11 149's line is now verse 9, and verse 1 is the root itself. "Human" enters at 14, on evidence.
- **Declared readings said plainly.** Identity with the body (19), mind as the body's organization told in the first person (20–21), and Nature as God (66, 153–154) are each said in verse to be readings, not findings.
- **Wholeness rebuilt.** v11's two dimensions (a sorting boundary, and low conflict) are gone from the core. v12 states D-whole as it is: seven works, graded 0/1/2, degree = lowest grade, members by fate and function, levels, self-production, sameness (38–52).
- **A3 as a test.** Anchors, negative controls and hard cases, the robot, and today's AI systems (68–78).
- **PR1** with its three strengths of requirement and memory's two claims (79–87). Jason's pattern line is verse 80.
- **A4 row by row**, with the take-apart test (88–100). It says only "the society I live in."
- **P1 and P2** as two separate chapters, matching the new numbering: P1 is the seam (101–109), P2 the collective mind (110–119).
- **A chapter on the first person** (120–128), giving consciousness weight as the preface promises: the hard problem undecided, the first person neither ghost nor illusion on the declared reading, the machine case, and Jason's "subjective" argument.
- **Freedom** (135–152) keeps v11's thesis verses, says openly that the core has no freedom entry, and rests the thesis on A2's declared reading. "Every event is caused" (135) is marked ‡ as a candidate, not presented as shown.
- **Vocabulary.** A2 says the book does not use "inside" for either description, so v12 says "first person / third person" throughout (121, 136, 137, 139, 173). "Part" now means member only, as D-whole, Definition 9 requires (65, 105).
