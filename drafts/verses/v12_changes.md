# Changes from v11 to v12 (draft)

v12 is not an edit of v11. It is derived again from core v2 (`entries/`, promoted 2026-10-15), in the core's order, and keeps v11's verses only where they still fit. Numbering is new. v11 is unchanged.

## What's new

- **Order.** v11 opened with God, or Nature, and reached the self at verse 56. v12 opens where core v2 does: "I exist" (1), then what the I is (A2), then works, wholes, Nature, the test, the pattern, society, levels, the collective mind, then the first person, freedom, God or Nature, and the close.
- **The root.** A1 is "I exist." v11 149's line is now verse 9, and verse 1 is the root itself. "Human" enters at 14, on evidence.
- **Declared readings said plainly.** Identity with the body (19), mind as the body's organization told in the first person (20–21), and Nature as God (58, 145–146) are each said in verse to be readings, not findings.
- **Wholeness rebuilt.** v11's two dimensions (a sorting boundary, and low conflict) are gone from the core. v12 states D-whole as it is: seven works, graded 0/1/2, degree = lowest grade, members by fate and function, levels, self-production, sameness (38–52).
- **A3 as a test.** Anchors, negative controls and hard cases, the robot, and today's AI systems (60–70).
- **PR1** with its three strengths of requirement and memory's two claims (71–79). Jason's pattern line is verse 72.
- **A4 row by row**, with the take-apart test (80–92). It says only "the society I live in."
- **P1 and P2** as two separate chapters, matching the new numbering: P1 is the seam (93–101), P2 the collective mind (102–111).
- **A chapter on the first person** (112–120), giving consciousness weight as the preface promises: the hard problem undecided, the first person neither ghost nor illusion on the declared reading, the machine case, and Jason's "subjective" argument.
- **Freedom** (127–144) keeps v11's thesis verses, says openly that the core has no freedom entry, and rests the thesis on A2's declared reading. "Every event is caused" (127) is marked ‡ as a candidate, not presented as shown.
- **Vocabulary.** A2 says the book does not use "inside" for either description, so v12 says "first person / third person" throughout (113, 128, 129, 131, 165). "Part" now means member only, as D-whole, Definition 9 requires (57, 97).

## Self-check: notable fixes

Each verse was read against its entry for overclaiming and for nonsense (meaningless, circular, equivocal, unshown-as-shown, wrong).

1. **v11 148, "inside an order that doesn't end."** D-exist leaves open whether anything exists eternally, so this overclaimed. 157 now says only "Death is a whole's term ending. The river ends; the water goes on."
2. **"Nature aims at nothing" / "no plans."** Not in D-Nature as a finding; now tagged and worded as the declared reading (145–146). An earlier draft said "their desires are caused," which leaned on the ‡ candidate 127; removed.
3. **"A human being can't fail to be a part of Nature."** Equivocal under v2, where "part" means member and nothing is a member of Nature. Now "I can't fail to be in Nature" (57).
4. **Halbwachs.** An argument, not a finding; 87 now says "Halbwachs argued."
5. **The machine verse.** "If a machine says 'I exist' and means it" was equivocal ("means it" could mean "is sincere" or "is doubting"). 118 now asks whether the sentence is certain *for it*, which is A1 Scholium 1's question.
6. **"Inside."** Every use as the felt side was replaced, because A2 explicitly keeps the word out of its two descriptions.
7. **Membership.** v11 17's "to be a member is to share a fate" left out D-whole's function clause; 43 now says fate and function.
8. **A4 scope.** No society verse says "every people" or "a society" in general as a finding; the claim is "the society I live in" (80, 90). 163 keeps v11's "perhaps."
9. **127.** "Every event is caused" is no longer stated flat; it is marked ‡, with what would lower it in the note.
10. **72.** Jason's "function follows necessity" uses the modal word where PR1 requires "requirement" (PR1's "Two words"). Written with "requirement"; flagged below.
