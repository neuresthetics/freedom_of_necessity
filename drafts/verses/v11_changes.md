# Changes from v10 to v11

Every change below comes from Lens's fallacy audit of v10, copied into the repo at [`audits/v10_fallacy_audit.md`](audits/v10_fallacy_audit.md). The audit read v10 at commit 2b6a3e3, which includes the revised verse 152. Each entry gives the verse, the audit section it comes from, the reason (from the audit, in short), and the old and new wording of every passage changed: the verse, its note, and any glossary or front-matter line that had to match. Where Lens's suggested wording was not used exactly, the entry says so under **Deviation**. Verse numbers are unchanged from v10. v10 itself is unchanged. No scores were added.

**Count.** 26 verses changed in their text: 4, 5, 7, 25, 35, 37, 46, 55, 83, 85, 91, 92, 95, 102, 104, 110, 112, 124, 130, 138, 145, 148, 152, 159, 160, 161. 4 changed in their notes only: 56, 64, 82, 149. That makes 30 verses in all: the 24 flags (25 verses, since 56 and 149 share one), the factual fixes to 25, 37 and 145 (35 is both a flag and a factual fix), the optional note to 64, and the one-word caution at 124.

**Not changed.** The other minor cautions in the audit's PASSED list need more than one word (39: harder controls, such as a firm, a city or an ant colony; 127: studies of reconciliation, not famous figures), so they are left as they were and are not open items here.

**Deviation.** Lens's wording put a cross-reference, "(76)", in the verse. Verses carry no citations or cross-references, so it went to note 4. The gloss was moved so that it attaches to "function" and not to "caused desire".
