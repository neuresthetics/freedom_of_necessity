# Coherence check: Axioms of Necessity v12 against core v2

*Checked 2026-10-15. Draft, local only. The verse file and the core were not edited.*

## Verdict

Chapters I to X (the start, body, work, whole, Nature, the test, pattern, society, levels, collective mind) are coherent with the core. They are one chain, every tag is correct, the terms are consistent, and readings are marked as readings. The chain breaks at XI to XIII. The first-person and freedom chapters rest on a thesis and on Spinoza reports that no core entry carries, and one verse (133) presents part of that thesis as if D-Nature and D-whole supported it. The other problems are small: wording, missing tags, and forward references.

The word "fractal" appears nowhere. "Inside" for the felt side is gone, but three leftover uses are listed below. No split tracks.

**Issues: 19 in total. 2 high, 8 medium, 9 low.**

## High

**H1. Verse 133: the claim goes beyond its tags and conflicts with 132.**
Current: "Every whole takes its energy from something else. No whole that can be graded is free without degree. Finite things are free by degrees. [D-Nature, D-whole]"
Problem: D-Nature, Scholium 4 says every graded whole is *constrained* (E1D7). No entry says finite things are free by degrees. Verse 132 has just said that only Nature is free. With these tags, the verse turns the thesis into a finding.
Fix: "Every whole takes its energy from something else, so in the strict sense none is free. *That finite things are free by degrees is this book's thesis, not a finding.*" [D-Nature, D-whole; thesis]

**H2. Chapters XI to XIII have no entry to stand on.**
Verses 113, 128, 129, 131, 134 to 141 and 165 rest on "thesis". Verse 127 rests on a candidate axiom ("Every event is caused"). The connective verses (121 to 126) rest on bare scholia. The chain reaches freedom only through A2's declared reading plus Spinoza, so the whole freedom chapter is capped at "assumed". The file says this openly, so it is honest, but it is the one place where the chain does not run like the *Ethics*.
Fix: this is the open question already put to Jason. Either (a) add a freedom entry and an "every event is caused" entry to the core, or (b) keep the chapter, and label its header and the 165 close: "assumed, not derived; the chain proves nothing past P2." Until he decides, add this line under the XIII header: *"What follows is the book's thesis, held on A2's declared reading. No entry yet derives it."*

## Medium

**M1. Verses 93, 94, 97: "I" without the reading qualifier.** P1 proves the claim for *this body* and extends it to "I" only on A2's declared reading (P1 step 8, Scholium 4). Verse 98 says this, but only after 93 and 97 have stated "I am…" flatly.
Fix for 93: "This body is a whole at one level and a part at the next; on my reading, so am I." Or move 98 to sit directly after 93.

**M2. Verses 113 and 128 ("Neither outranks the other"): the reading is used without the tag.** Both rely on the two-descriptions reading (A2(b), marked a reading in 20 and 114) but are tagged [A2, thesis].
Fix: tag both [A2, reading; thesis].

**M3. Verses 72 and 163: "the pattern does not change" claims more than PR1 allows.** PR1 sorts its requirements into three strengths, some of which hold only in some circumstances (verse 76 says so). PR1 also adds no confidence.
Fix for 72: "The expression changes completely at each scale; where the requirement holds, the pattern does not." Fix for 163: "…in forms that change completely at each scale. Where the requirement holds, the pattern holds."

**M4. Verse 89 is garbled and misstates A4.** Current: "'To a degree' means at least one on every work, and two on none claimed."
Fix (A4: "does not claim grade 2 on any row"): "'To a degree' means a grade of at least one on every work. A grade of two is claimed on none. It is not a claim that a society is an organism."

**M5. Chapter XII (121 to 126) floats free of the core.** It has no entry tag except 122 (A2) and 123 (D-work), yet 126 and 140 (passions becoming actions) and 148 (the third kind of knowing) depend on it.
Fix: label the chapter as a scholium on A2 ("reports, not steps"), or carry it into the freedom entry if one is made (see H2).

**M6. Verse 148 rests on the Nature-as-God reading but is not tagged as a reading.** Current: "…the intellectual love of God… [D-Nature, scholium]".
Fix: [D-Nature, reading; scholium].

**M7. Chapter IV header: "*Inside Nature, some things hold together, and by degrees.*"** It uses "inside", and it uses Nature one chapter before Nature is defined (V).
Fix: "*Some things hold together, and by degrees.*"

**M8. Verse 36 uses society and quarantine (A4, ch. VIII) in the Work chapter.** It is a forward reference, and 86 repeats it.
Fix: use a body example in 36 ("Take a body apart, keep each cell fed, and each still guards its own membrane. What stops is the immune response."), or tag 36 [D-work; A4, ahead].
