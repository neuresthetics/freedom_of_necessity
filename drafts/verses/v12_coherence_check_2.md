# Coherence check 2: Axioms of Necessity v12 against core v2

*Second, independent pass, 2026-10-15. Done before reading `v12_coherence_check.md`, then compared. Draft, local only. Neither the verse file nor the core was edited.*

## Verdict

The chain is one unified order, like the *Ethics*, with no split tracks and no "fractal". Chapters I–X mostly hold, but not as cleanly as the first pass said ("every tag is correct"). Four verses there overclaim or slip: 13, 53, 69 and 102. The freedom block (XI–XIII) is the weak link, as the first pass found. It also has a second gap: its confidence should be capped by the ‡ in verse 127, but its tags don't show it. The method chapter (XV) comes after verses that already use its rules.

**Issues: 24 in total. 2 high, 9 medium, 13 low.** I confirm both of the first pass's highs and most of its mediums. 14 issues are new.

## High (confirmed from pass 1)

- **H1. Verse 132 against 133.** Same as pass 1 H1. Its middle sentence is also garbled: "No whole that can be graded is free without degree." Pass 1's fix stands.
- **H2. XI–XIII have no entry.** Same as pass 1 H2. See N9 for the cap that goes with it.

## Where I disagree with pass 1

- **Verdict on I–X ("every tag is correct").** No: see N1–N5.
- **L7 (verse 70).** No fix is needed. Definitions are not claims (house rule 1), so "the start is the one exception" is already exact.
- **M8 (verse 36), body fix.** "Each cell still guards its own membrane; what stops is the immune response" is weak, because immune cells also act each for itself. Prefer the alternative pass 1 offered: keep the society example and tag it [D-work; A4, ahead].
- **L5 (verse 156).** Partly. A thing whose work stops altogether has degree 0 and is no whole at all. That is D-whole Def. 8, not Def. 12's ending. Better: "A whole ends when its organization comes to a full stop."
- I agree with all the other findings: M1–M7, L1–L4, L6, L8 and L9.

## Checks passed

No "fractal". "Inside" for the felt side is gone; the leftovers are the IV header, 120 and 142, as in pass 1 (66's "inside one [a cell]" is literal and fine). "Part" means member except in reports (50, 117) and P1's title wording (93). The 0/1/2 grades and the lowest-grade rule are right (38, 39, 89 wording aside). The declared readings at 19–21, 58, 98, 114, 145 and 146 are marked. Nature is not a whole and has no members (55, 56, 101).
