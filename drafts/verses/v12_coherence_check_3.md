## Verdict

v12 is still one unified chain, and the opening → close holds at the level of A1 (1, 9, 164). This pass looked at the whole-book argument, and that's where it found the one new structural gap: **A3 and A4 together require that the society produces itself, and no verse or entry says so** (N1). The rest is redundancy, entry-vs-verse strength, undefined lowercase "nature," and unmarked norms. Both prior highs are confirmed.

**New issues: 14. 1 high, 6 medium, 7 low.** Prior passes: 2 high, 15 medium, ~20 low. New finds per pass: 19 → 18 → 14, and the severity is dropping too. Chapters I–X are converging, but the structural layer only came into view this time.
